ingredients:
  - items:
      - "2 egg whites"
      - "1/2 tsp. vanilla"
      - "1 cup coconut"
      - "1 cup white sugar"
      - "1 Tbsp. flour"
      - "2 cups cornflakes"
---
1. Whip egg whites until stiff, add 1 Tbsp. flour & whip 2-3 minutes longer.
2. Fold in sugar, add flavoring, cornflakes, & coconut.
3. Drop onto parchment covered cookie sheets by spoonfuls and bake at 350°F for 15-20 minutes.
4. Cool on wire rack.

**Note:** Use glass bowl or metal bowl, not plastic or rubber bowl.
