ingredients:
  - items:
      - "2 cups crushed pineapple"
      - "1 cup sugar"
      - "2 Tbsp. Knox flavored gelatin"
      - "3/4 cups grated American cheese"
      - "1/2 pint whipping cream (unwhipped)"
      - "1 cup black walnuts (chopped)"
      - "Juice of 1 lemon"
---
1. Soak gelatin in 1/2 cup cold water.
2. Heat pineapple and add sugar and lemon juice.
3. Stir in gelatin, and continue stirring until gelatin dissolves.
4. When the mixture begins to set add cheese and nuts.
5. Chill for several hours.
