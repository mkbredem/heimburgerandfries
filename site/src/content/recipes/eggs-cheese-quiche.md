ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 Pillsbury Pie Crust"
      - "1 sweet onion diced and sauteed in butter"
      - "12 slices of bacon - crisply cooked"
      - "5 eggs (beaten thoroughly with half & half)"
      - "2 1/2 cup half & half (beaten thoroughly with eggs)"
      - "2 cups shredded monterey jack cheese"
---
1. Place crust in 9" pie pan.
2. Place sauteed onions over crust.
3. Next, place crumbled bacon over crust.
4. Then place shredded cheese over bacon.
5. Finally, pour eggs & half & half mixture over cheese.
6. Bake at 350°F for about 1 hour.
7. Quiche is done when knife is placed in center of quiche and it comes out clean.
8. Let sit for about 10 minutes until serving.
