ingredients:
  - items:
      - "3/4 cup canola oil"
      - "1 cup sugar"
      - "2 tsp. baking soda"
      - "1/2 tsp. cloves"
      - "1/2 tsp. salt"
      - "1/4 cup molasses"
      - "1 egg"
      - "2 cups flour"
      - "1/2 tsp. ginger"
      - "1 tsp. cinnamon"
---
1. Mix well oil, sugar, molasses, and egg.
2. Sift together flour, soda, salt, and spices; add to first mixture.
3. Mix well and chill.
4. Form into 1 inch balls, roll in sugar, and place on greased cookie sheet 2" apart.
5. Bake at 375°F for 8-10 minutes.
