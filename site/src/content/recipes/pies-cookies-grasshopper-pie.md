  - group: "Crust"
    items:
      - "1 already prepared chocolate crust"
      - "or 30 crushed chocolate wafers"
      - "3 Tbsp. butter melted"
  - group: "Filling"
    items:
      - "24 large marshmallows"
      - "2/3 cup milk"
      - "1/4 cup creme de menthe"
      - "2 Tbsp. creme de cocoa"
      - "1 cup Cool Whip"
---
1. Mix cookie crumbs and butter and press into a 9" pie pan.
2. In a double boiler, melt 24 large marshmallows with 2/3 cup milk.
3. Let cool, then add 1/4 cup creme de menthe and 2 Tbsp. creme de cocoa.
4. Let cool until thickened, then add 1 cup Cool Whip.
5. Cover and store in freezer.
