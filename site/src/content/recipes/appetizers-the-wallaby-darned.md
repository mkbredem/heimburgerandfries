ingredients:
  - items:
      - "8 oz. frozen sliced peaches"
      - "1/2 cup Bacardi frozen fuzzy navel mix"
      - "1/2 cup ice"
      - "1/2 cup Champagne"
      - "2 shots water"
      - "1 shot peach schnapps"
      - "1 shot vodka"
      - "1 tablespoon sugar"
---
1. Combine all ingredients in a blender.
2. Blend on high for 30 seconds or until ice is completely crushed and the drink is smooth.
