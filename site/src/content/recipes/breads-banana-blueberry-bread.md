      - "1 stick butter"
      - "3/4 cup sugar"
      - "2 eggs"
      - "1 tsp. vanilla"
      - "5-6 mashed bananas"
      - "2 cups flour"
      - "1/4 tsp. salt"
      - "1 tsp. baking soda"
      - "1 1/2 - 2 cups fresh or frozen blueberries"
---
1. Cream butter and sugar thoroughly.
2. Add 2 eggs, 1 tsp. vanilla, and 5-6 mashed bananas.
3. Mix in 2 cups flour, 1/4 tsp. salt, and 1 tsp. baking soda.
4. Finally, add 1 1/2 - 2 cups fresh or frozen blueberries.
5. Pour into 4 small, greased/floured bread pans.
6. Bake at 325°F-350°F for 1 hour.
