  - items:
      - "4 whole chicken breasts"
      - "medium sliced sweet onion"
      - "salt & pepper to taste"
---
1. Place chicken breasts, onion, and salt & pepper in a stock pot. Fill the pot half-full of water and cook for about 1 1/2 hours. When the chicken falls away from the bone, the breasts are done.
2. Take chicken from the bone, cut in bite-sized pieces, and return to the broth.
3. You may need to add a little water (you need about 1/2 pot).
4. When the liquid begins to boil, add the noodles and cook for about 10-15 minutes or until they are tender.
