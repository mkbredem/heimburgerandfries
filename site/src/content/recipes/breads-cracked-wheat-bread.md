      - "5 - 5 1/2 cup flour (we use 1 cup whole wheat, 4 1/2 unbleached flour and 2 Tbsp. wheat germ)"

1. Combine boiling water, cracked wheat, brown sugar, butter, and salt; cool until warm.
2. Meanwhile, soften yeast in warm water and combine with above.
3. Beat in as much flour as you can, then knead in the rest. Knead 8-10 min.
4. Let rise 1 1/2 hrs. Punch down, divide in two, let rest 10 min.
5. Roll each piece into a 12x16 shape, then roll up jelly-roll style into a 12" loaf.
6. Pinch ends and tuck under. Let rise 1 hr. on a greased cookie sheet.
7. Bake 375°F for 30 min.

**Note:** This is so yummy fresh out of the oven, or later when cooled and the next morning as toast!

**Note:** A Great Summer Sandwich: assemble as follows - sliced cracked wheat bread, thinly sliced rare roast beef, garden fresh sliced tomatoes, dollop of homemade mayo and topped with dill weed.
