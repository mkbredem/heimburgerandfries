ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 box Bakery Style Blueberry Muffin Mix"
      - "1 Tbsp. poppyseeds"
      - "1 egg"
      - "3/4 cup water"
      - "1 Tbsp. lemon zest"
      - "Blueberries and raspberries to taste (about 3/4 cup of each)"
  - group: "Glaze"
    items:
      - "1/2 cup powdered sugar"
      - "1 Tbsp. lemon juice"
      - "1 Tbsp. lemon zest"
---
1. Mix above ingredients until smooth.
2. Add blueberries and raspberries to your particular taste (I use about 3/4 cup of each).
3. Before baking, add crumb topping over mixture.
4. Bake in 3 greased/floured small bread pans at 350°F for 50-60 minutes.
5. Cool on cooling racks and top with glaze made from 1/2 cup powdered sugar, 1 Tbsp. lemon juice, and 1 Tbsp. lemon zest.

**Note:** Before baking, add crumb topping over mixture.
