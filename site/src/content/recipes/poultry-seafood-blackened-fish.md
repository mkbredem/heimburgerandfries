  - group: "Fish"
    items:
      - "2 lbs. filet skinned (Salmon, Grouper)"
      - "Olive oil, or butter, or cooking spray (flavored)"
      - "Blackened seasoning"
  - group: "Blackened Seasoning Mix"
    items:
      - "1/2 Tablespoon each of: paprika, onion powder, garlic powder, thyme, oregano, celery seed, and basil"
      - "1/4 tsp. each of: cayenne pepper, black pepper, white pepper, salt"
---
1. Fish: Dredge fish in season mix.
2. Fish: Brush with olive oil to moisten.
3. Fish: Grill over medium heat 8-10 min. for salmon or until fish flakes easily.
4. Blackened Seasoning Mix: Blend all ingredients well.
