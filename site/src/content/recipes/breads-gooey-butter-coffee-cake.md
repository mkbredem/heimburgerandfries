ingredients:
  - group: "Bottom layer"
    items:
      - "1 box Duncan Hines Yellow Cake Mix"
      - "1/2 cup (1 stick) melted butter"
      - "2 eggs"
  - group: "Top layer"
    items:
      - "8 oz. cream cheese"
      - "1 box powdered sugar"
      - "2 well-beaten eggs"
---
1. Mix cake mix, melted butter, and eggs.
2. Spread above ingredients in greased and floured 9"x12" pan.
3. Mix together cream cheese, powdered sugar, and eggs. Spread over bottom layer.
4. Sprinkle with powdered sugar and bake for 40-50 minutes at 350°F.
