ingredients:
  - items:
      - "6-8 potatoes (peeled and sliced)"
      - "salt & pepper to taste"
      - "4 Tbsp. margarine"
      - "milk"
---
1. Place sliced potatoes in a greased baking dish, salt & pepper, cover with milk and top with margarine.
2. Bake covered at 350°F for 1 hour.
3. Uncover and continue baking for another 30 minutes.
