  - items:
      - "3 Tbls. butter"
      - "3 cups sliced or minced onions - or leek is preferred"
      - "3 Tbls. flour"
      - "1 quart stock and about 1 qt. milk (or chicken stock + milk)"
      - "1 Tbl. salt"
      - "Pepper to taste"
      - "4 cups (about 1 1/2 lbs.) potatoes"
      - "1/3 to 1/2 cup sour cream and 2 to 3 Tbls. butter"
      - "2 to 3 Tbls. minced parsley or chives"
---
1. Melt butter over moderate heat and stir in onions. Cover pan and cook slowly for about 5 min. without browning.
2. Blend in the flour and stir for about 2 minutes to cook the flour without browning it.
3. Remove from heat and let it cool a minute - then gradually beat in a cup or so of stock. Blend thoroughly with the onions and flour and then add the rest of the water. (Do not add the milk at this time or it will curdle.)
4. Stir in the salt, pepper and potatoes. Bring to boiling and simmer partially covered for about 40 minutes until vegetables are tender.
5. When you are ready to serve add the milk and return to a simmer. Blend in as much of the cream as you wish. Add butter. Taste carefully adding more salt and pepper as needed.
6. Garnish with a spoonful of cream and a sprinkling of herbs.
