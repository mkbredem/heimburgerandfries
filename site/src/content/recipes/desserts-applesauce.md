      - "Water"
      - "Heavy saucepan"
---
1. Peel, quarter, and core apples.
2. Place in saucepan with about 1 inch of water in pan.
3. Cook covered over medium heat.
4. Cook until apples are almost liquid (may need to add a little more water depending upon apples).
5. Add enough sugar or Equal to suit you.
6. Chill.
