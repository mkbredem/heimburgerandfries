  - group: "Beef and Noodles"
    items:
      - "2 1/2 - 3 pound piece of London Broil"

1. Homemade Noodles: Roll very thin on a floured surface; let stand 20 minutes.
2. Homemade Noodles: Roll up loosely; let slices be 1/4" wide, unroll, spread out and let dry about 2 hours. (If desired you may store the noodles in an airtight container in the freezer until needed.)
3. Beef and Noodles: Flour and salt and pepper meat. Brown in a small amount of oil (use dutch oven).
4. Beef and Noodles: Reduce heat, cover with a sliced onion, and simmer for about 2 hours or until meat is tender. (Keep checking because you may need to add water if the meat doesn't draw its own juices.)
5. Beef and Noodles: When meat is tender, remove from broth, cut in bite-sized pieces and return to broth.
6. Beef and Noodles: Add water to about half of dutch oven, cook uncovered. When liquid bubbles, drop in noodles and cook uncovered for about 10-15 minutes.
