ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 pound cleaned large mushrooms"
      - "small sweet onion chopped finely"
      - "5 slices of white bread (crusts removed) cubed"
      - "1/4 - 1/2 cup margarine"
      - "mozzarella cheese - 16 oz"
---
1. After cleaning mushrooms carefully, remove stems and place in food processor - chop finely.
2. Next, chop onion finely.
3. Then melt margarine in a large skillet over medium heat. Add mushrooms and onion. Saute until tender.
4. Then add bread cubes, and mix until moisture is absorbed.
5. Finally, add about 12 oz of mozzarella cheese and mix.
6. Stuff mushrooms and top with mozzarella.
7. Bake for 20 minutes in Pyrex baking dish (dotted with margarine slices) at 350°F.
8. Then broil for 3 minutes to brown.
