ingredients:
  - items:
      - "McIntosh or Granny Smith apples"
      - "1 stick margarine"
      - "1 cup sugar"
      - "1 cup flour"
      - "1/4 tsp. salt"
      - "1 tsp cinnamon"
---
1. Peel and core enough apples that when quartered and sliced will fill 9"x11" pan 1/2 way up the sides.
2. In a bowl cut shortening (margarine) into flour, sugar, salt and cinnamon (mixture should be crumbly).
3. Place about 1/2" water in pan with apples, then sprinkle mixture above over the apples.
4. Bake for 1 hour at 350°F.
