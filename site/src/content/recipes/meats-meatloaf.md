ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 pound ground sirloin"
      - "1 small sweet onion - diced finely"
      - "1 egg - beaten"
      - "cornflakes - about 1 1/2 cups"
      - "milk"
---
1. Place sirloin in a bowl, add onion, egg, and salt & pepper. Mix.
2. Add cornflakes, and enough milk to make meatloaf the right consistency.
3. Shape into a loaf, and place in loaf pan (put enough water in pan to cover the bottom of the pan).
4. If desired, place 1 piece of bacon on top to flavor, or you may cover the top with catsup.
5. Bake about 1 hour at 350°F.
