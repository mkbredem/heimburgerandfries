ingredients:
  - items:
      - "4 pieces boneless skinless chicken"
      - "1/2 stick margarine melted"
      - "8 oz. grated Parmesan cheese"
---
1. Brush chicken pieces with margarine, and roll in grated Parmesan cheese.
2. Bake in an 8" x 8" greased baking dish at 350°F for 1 hour.
