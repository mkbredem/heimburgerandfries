1. Cook 4 whole chicken breasts in large stock pot (1/2 pot of water).
2. Also, cook with 1 sweet onion sliced.
3. Cook about 1 1/2 hours, or until chicken is tender.
4. Remove chicken, cut into bite-sized pieces.
5. Add 3 cups homemade or frozen noodles.
6. Then add 1 can drained green beans, 1/2 package frozen peas, 2 cups shredded carrots and simmer for about 2 hours.
7. You may need to add additional water, and salt & pepper.
