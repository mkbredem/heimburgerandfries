      - "core 3 apples"

      - "1 package cherry Jell-O"
      - "1 package strawberry Jell-O"
      - "Juices from ground apples and oranges"
      - "1-2 cups sugar"
---
1. Grind apples & oranges together (reserve liquid) and place ground fruit in bowl.
2. Grind cranberries and add 1-2 cups sugar.
3. Place both Jell-Os in a bowl and add boiling liquid to dissolve Jell-O.
4. Fold in oranges, apples, and cranberries, + add sugar.
5. Place in mold and chill.
