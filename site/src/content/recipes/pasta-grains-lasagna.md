ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 pound ground sirloin"
      - "1 large sweet onion - chopped finely"
      - "1 small head of garlic (minced finely)"
      - "1/4 tsp. pepper"
      - "1 tsp. salt"
      - "32 oz. whole tomatoes - chopped"
      - "2 - 6 oz cans tomato paste"
      - "2 eggs"
      - "1 1/2 lb. mozzarella cheese"
      - "16 oz ricotta cheese"
      - "1 lb. lasagna noodles - cooked"
---
1. In a skillet brown ground sirloin with onion and garlic; drain grease off meat.
2. Add chopped tomatoes, tomato paste, pepper, and salt. Simmer about 1 hour.
3. In a large bowl, mix ricotta cheese and eggs.
4. In a 9"x13" lasagna pan place a layer of lasagna noodles on the bottom, top with 1/2 of sauce, then 1/2 of ricotta mixture, 3/4 lb. mozzarella, Parmesan cheese.
5. Repeat above mixture for the next layer.
6. Bake about 1 hour at 350°F.
