ingredients:
  - group: "Marinade"
    items:
      - "Juice of 3 lemons"
      - "3/4 cup canola oil"
      - "3/4 cup catsup"
      - "garlic powder (as much as desired)"
      - "worcestershire sauce"
  - group: "Kabobs"
    items:
      - "sirloin sizzlers"
      - "whole mushrooms"
      - "bacon"
      - "onion slices"
---
1. Mix all of the marinade ingredients.
2. Marinate sirloin sizzlers whole or cut into large chunks - marinate for 1-2 days. Also, add whole mushrooms.
3. To make kabobs, thread 3 pieces of meat, 1 square of bacon, mushroom, onion slice. Repeat until skewer is as full as desired.
4. Grill 15-20 minutes, brushing with sauce frequently.
