ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 1/2 cups pumpkin"
      - "1 cup brown sugar"
      - "1 Tbsp. molasses"
      - "1 Tbsp. melted butter"
      - "2 well-beaten eggs"
      - "2 cups milk"
      - "1/4 tsp. salt"
      - "1 tsp. cinnamon"
      - "1/4 tsp. ginger"
      - "1/4 tsp. nutmeg"
---
1. Heat milk and add butter.
2. Mix the spices with the sugar.
3. Then add the molasses, pumpkin, and milk.
4. Next, add the eggs.
5. Pour into unbaked crust.
6. Bake at 400°F for 15 minutes, reduce heat to 350°F, and continue baking for another 45 minutes, or until custard is firm.

**Note:** Place knife in center of pie; if it comes out clean, the custard is cooked.
