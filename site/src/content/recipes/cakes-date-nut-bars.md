      - "1 tsp. vanilla"
      - "3 eggs"
      - "1 1/2 cup flour"
      - "1/2 cup milk"
      - "1 cup sugar"

1. Beat sugar & eggs.
2. Add other ingredients.
3. Bake in greased 8"x10" pan for 45 min - 1 hr. at 3[?]5°F.
4. After baking, sprinkle with powdered sugar & cut into squares.
