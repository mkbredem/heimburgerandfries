ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 1/2 pound ground sirloin"
      - "2/3 cup carnation milk"
      - "1 egg - beaten"
      - "1 small onion - diced"
      - "salt & pepper"
      - "3 crackers - crushed"
---
1. Mix all of the above ingredients together.
2. Slice a loaf of French Bread lengthwise.
3. Spread above mixture onto sliced bread.
4. Bake on a cookie sheet at 350°F for 40-45 minutes.
5. Top with Velveeta Cheese and continue baking until cheese melts.
