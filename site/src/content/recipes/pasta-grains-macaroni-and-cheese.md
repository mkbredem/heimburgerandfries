ingredients:
  - items:
      - "3 cups macaroni (elbow)"
      - "1 1/2 pounds grated colby cheese"
      - "milk"
      - "margarine"
      - "salt and pepper to taste"
---
1. Place macaroni in boiling salted water in a medium saucepan. Cook only 3-4 minutes (macaroni should be slightly hard; if you cook too long it won't absorb the milk).
2. Divide macaroni into halves. Place 1/2 into 8"x10" greased casserole, and salt and pepper. Cover generously with grated cheese.
3. Place next layer of macaroni onto cheese and apply salt & pepper. Top with remainder of cheese. Dot with margarine slices.
4. Add milk until it comes up to the top of cheese (but don't cover the cheese).
5. Bake at 350°F for about 1 hour.
