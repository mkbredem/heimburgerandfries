ingredients:
  - items:
      - "4 cups chicken broth"
      - "1 - 9 oz pkg. cheese tortellini"
      - "1 - 15 oz. cannellini beans, drained"
      - "1 - 15 oz can diced tomatoes (with Italian flavorings)"
      - "1/2 cup shredded fresh basil"
      - "2 Tbsp. balsamic vinegar"
      - "salt to taste"
      - "1 1/2 tsp fresh ground pepper"
      - "1/3 cup shredded Parmesan cheese"
---
1. In a large pot, bring broth to a boil, add tortellini, & cook for 4 minutes.
2. Stir in beans and tomatoes. Reduce heat and simmer for 5 minutes.
3. Remove from heat, stir in basil, balsamic vinegar, and salt.
4. Ladle into bowls, sprinkle with Parmesan cheese, & pepper.
