ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 cup butter"
      - "2 eggs"
      - "1 cup oil"
      - "1 cup powdered sugar"
      - "1 cup granulated sugar"
      - "1 tsp. cream of tartar"
      - "1 tsp. baking soda"
      - "1/4 tsp. salt"
      - "4 cups flour"
      - "1 tsp. vanilla"
---
1. Cream oil, butter, and both sugars.
2. Add vanilla and eggs.
3. Sift dry ingredients and add to creamed mixture.
4. Roll tsp. of dough into a ball, roll ball in granulated sugar, and press down on greased cookie sheet with a glass that has been dipped in sugar.
5. Press with fork and bake at 350°F for 10-12 minutes.
