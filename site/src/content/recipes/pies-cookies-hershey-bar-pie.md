ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 Oreo crust"
      - "1 16 oz Hershey w/ Almond Bar"
      - "16 oz Cool Whip - thawed"
---
1. Break bar into small pieces and melt in microwave or in a double-boiler on the stove.
2. Gradually fold melted Hershey Bar into 8 oz of Cool Whip.
3. Pour mixture into crust.
4. Top with remaining Cool Whip and chocolate shavings.
5. Refrigerate.
