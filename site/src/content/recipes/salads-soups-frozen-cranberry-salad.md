ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 pound ground cranberries"
      - "2 cups ground apples"
      - "1 pound miniature marshmallows"
      - "1 cup sugar"
      - "16 oz. container Cool Whip"
---
1. Mix ground cranberries, apples, and sugar.
2. Place in a bowl and refrigerate overnight.
3. Add Cool Whip and marshmallows.
4. Freeze.
