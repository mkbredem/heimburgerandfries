ingredients:
  - items:
      - "2 cups milk (scalded)"
      - "1 Tbsp. sugar"
      - "2 tsp. salt"
      - "yeast (1 cake fresh or 1 pkg. yeast)"
      - "1/4 cup lukewarm water"
      - "1/2 cup shortening"
      - "2 tsp. grated lemon peel"
      - "3/4 cup sugar"
      - "1 tsp. vanilla"
      - "1/4 tsp. nutmeg"
      - "2 eggs (beaten)"
      - "3-4 cups flour"
      - "3/4 cup chopped almonds"
      - "2 cups seedless raisins (light and dark)"
      - "1 cup chopped candied fruit (red and green cherries, pineapple)"
---
1. Combine milk, 1 Tbsp. sugar and salt; cool to lukewarm.
2. Add yeast that has been softened in lukewarm water.
3. Add 3 cups flour; beat thoroughly. Let rise until light and bubbly.
4. Thoroughly cream shortening and sugar. Add lemon peel, vanilla, nutmeg, and eggs.
5. Stir into sponge mixture.
6. Add remaining ingredients; mix to make smooth, soft dough. Let rest 10 minutes.
7. Knead on lightly floured surface until smooth and elastic.
8. Place in greased bowl; cover with a damp cloth and let rise until double.
9. Punch down. Shape loaves. Let rise until double.
10. Bake for 1 hour at 350°F.
