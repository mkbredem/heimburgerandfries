ingredients:
  - group: "Cookies"
    items:
      - "3/4 cup chopped dried apricots"
      - "3/4 cup butter"
      - "1 3/4 cup flour"
      - "1 cup brown sugar"
      - "1/2 cup sugar"
      - "1 egg"
      - "1 tsp. baking powder"
      - "1 tsp. vanilla"
      - "2 cups quick rolled oats"
      - "1/2 tsp. cinnamon"
      - "1/2 cup walnuts"
      - "1/4 tsp. baking soda"
  - group: "Icing"
    items:
      - "1 cup sifted powdered sugar"
      - "2-3 Tbsp. milk"
---
1. Chop apricots, cover with boiled water. Let stand 5 min. and drain.
2. Mix softened butter and add 1/2 of flour, brown sugar, sugar, eggs, baking powder, vanilla, cinnamon, & soda.
3. Beat until thoroughly mixed, stir in remaining ingredients.
4. Finally add oats, apricots & nuts.
5. Drop by tsp. onto ungreased cookie sheets.
6. Bake at 350°F about 10 min. or until edges are brown.
7. Place on wire rack to cool; drizzle with icing.
8. Icing: Mix powdered sugar and milk.
