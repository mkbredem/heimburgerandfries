ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 cup flour"
      - "1 cup sugar"
      - "2 Tbsp. baking powder"
      - "pinch of salt"
      - "2 egg yolks, beaten"
      - "2 Tbsp. melted Crisco"
      - "2 egg whites"
      - "sliced fresh peaches (sweetened)"
---
1. Mix flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
2. Add egg yolks and melted Crisco. Mix well.
3. Beat egg whites until stiff and fold in mixture.
4. Drop over sliced, fresh peaches (sweetened).
5. Bake at 350°F for 35-40 minutes.
