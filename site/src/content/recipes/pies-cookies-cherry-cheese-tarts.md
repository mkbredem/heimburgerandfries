ingredients:
  - items:
      - "3 - 8 oz pkgs. cream cheese"
      - "4 eggs"
      - "1 cup sugar"
      - "1 tsp. vanilla"
      - "24 vanilla wafers"
      - "Cherry Pie Filling - 1 can"
---
1. Beat cream cheese, eggs, sugar, and vanilla until smooth.
2. Put 24 cupcake papers in muffin tins. Place a vanilla wafer in each cupcake paper.
3. Divide batter and pour evenly over wafers.
4. Bake for 25 minutes at 350°F. (Color will be pale.)
5. Remove from papers and cool.
6. Place a small amount of cherry pie filling on each tart.
