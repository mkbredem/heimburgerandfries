      - "salt and pepper to taste"
  - group: "Dumplings"
    items:
      - "1 cup flour"
      - "1 1/2 tsp. baking powder"
      - "1/2 tsp. salt"
      - "3 Tbsp. Crisco"
      - "2/3 cup milk"
---
1. Cook chicken as directed for chicken and noodles. Place chicken (cut-up) in a separate bowl.
2. Place 2 1/2 cups chicken broth (lightly salted) in a 9x13 Pyrex baking dish. Microwave 6-8 minutes on high power or until liquid bubbles.
3. Then add: drop dumplings by spoonful onto boiling liquid.
4. Dumplings: Place flour, baking powder, and salt in mixing bowl. Cut in shortening until mixture looks like cornmeal. Add milk and stir until mixture is moist.
5. Microwave uncovered for 6 minutes on High.
6. Cover and microwave on High for 5 minutes.
