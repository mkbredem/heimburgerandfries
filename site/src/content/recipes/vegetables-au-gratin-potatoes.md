ingredients:
  - items:
      - "6-8 potatoes (peeled and sliced thinly)"
      - "1 large onion, thinly sliced"
      - "6, 1/4\" slices of Velveeta cheese"
      - "4 Tbsp. flour"
      - "milk"
      - "salt & pepper"
---
1. Place 1/2 of sliced potatoes in a large greased baking dish.
2. Salt & pepper to taste, cover with 3 slices of cheese, 1/2 of cheese slices, and top with 2 Tbsp. flour.
3. Then layer remaining potatoes, onions, cheese, and flour.
4. Cover with milk.
5. Bake covered at 350°F for 1 hour.
6. Uncover and bake for another 30 minutes.
