1. Scald 1 cup canned Milnot and fill Milnot can with water.
2. Place 1 stick margarine, 1/2 cup sugar and 1 tsp. salt in large bowl; pour hot milk over it. Let cool.
3. Add 1 tsp. vanilla and 2 beaten eggs.
4. Dissolve 1 pkg. yeast and 1/2 tsp. sugar in 1/4 cup lukewarm water.
5. Add 2 cups flour, then set aside to get bubbly.
6. Beat and add flour 1 cup at a time. Then finish adding flour with a spoon - using a total of about 5 cups flour.
7. Place on a floured bread board and knead until dough doesn't stick to hands. (Fold top down, rotate dough and knead.)
8. Place in greased bowl to rise and cover with a damp dish towel. (Make sure you flip dough so that both sides are greased.)
9. Punch down 2-3 times.
10. Brush with butter and sprinkle with sugar and cinnamon.
11. Bake at 350°F for 25-30 minutes.
