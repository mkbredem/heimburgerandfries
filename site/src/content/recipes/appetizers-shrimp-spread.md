ingredients:
  - items:
      - "3 oz. cream cheese"
      - "1 tsp. lemon juice"
      - "2 tsp. grated onion"
      - "1 can baby shrimp - cleaned and chopped"
      - "Worcestershire sauce - dash"
      - "salt and pepper"
---
1. Cream cheese with lemon juice.
2. Add onion, shrimp, Worcestershire, salt and pepper.
3. Chill and spread on crackers.
