ingredients:
  - items:
      - "6 oz. spaghetti"
      - "2 Tbsp. butter"
      - "1/3 cup grated fresh parmesan cheese"
      - "2 well-beaten eggs"
      - "1 cup cottage cheese"
      - "1 lb. ground sirloin"
      - "1/2 cup chopped sweet onion"
      - "1/4 cup chopped green pepper"
      - "16 oz tomato sauce"
      - "1/2 cup shredded mozzarella cheese"
---
1. Cook spaghetti according to package directions; stir butter into drained, hot spaghetti.
2. Stir in Parmesan cheese and eggs.
3. Form spaghetti mixture into a crust in a buttered 10" pie pan.
4. Spread cottage cheese over the bottom of the spaghetti crust.
5. In skillet, cook ground sirloin, onion, and green pepper until vegetables are tender and the meat is brown. Drain off excess fat.
6. Stir in tomato sauce, and heat thoroughly.
7. Turn meat mixture into crust.
8. Bake uncovered at 350°F for 20 min.
9. Sprinkle mozzarella cheese over the pie and cook for an additional 5 minutes.
