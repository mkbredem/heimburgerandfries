      - "2 medium Vidalia or sweet onions, chopped with food processor"
      - "1 bunch of garlic, chopped with food processor"
      - "2-3 Tbsp. olive oil"
      - "2 - 28 oz cans whole tomatoes"
      - "1 - 15 oz can whole tomatoes"
      - "2 - 12 oz cans tomato paste"
      - "3-4 cups hot water"
      - "2 Tbsp. sugar"
      - "2 tsp. salt"
      - "1 tsp. pepper"
  - group: "Meatballs"
    items:

      - "1/2 cup milk"

1. Sauce: Place a heated dutch oven (large pan) on stove. Place 2-3 Tbsp. olive oil in pan, and fold in chopped onion and garlic.
2. Sauce: Saute until onion is clear or transparent (not brown).
3. Sauce: Add 2 - 28 oz cans and 1 - 15 oz cans whole tomatoes (place tomatoes in food processor first).
4. Sauce: Then add 2 - 12 oz cans tomato paste.
5. Sauce: Next, pour about 3-4 cups of hot water into empty tomato paste cans, scraping sides. Add all water to sauce.
6. Sauce: Then add 2 Tbsp. sugar, 2 tsp. salt, and 1 tsp. pepper. Cook over low heat several hours (uncovered).
7. Meatballs: Mix well, form into balls, brown evenly in olive oil.
8. Meatballs: Simmer in sauce.
