1. Cream butter and sugars, and add egg and vanilla; mix.
2. Sift together flour, baking powder, salt, and baking soda; add alternately with milk, mixing well after each addition.
3. Stir in chocolate chips, cherries and nuts.
4. Place rounded teaspoons of dough on greased cookie sheets.
5. Bake at 375°F for 10-12 minutes.
