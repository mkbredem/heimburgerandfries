ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 1/2 pound ground sirloin"
      - "1/2 cup chopped onion"
      - "1/8 cup water"
      - "2 Tbsp. chopped green peppers"
      - "1/4 cup chopped celery"
      - "1 Tbsp. cornstarch"
      - "dash of garlic powder"
      - "1/4 cup catsup"
      - "1/2 cup tomato puree"
      - "1/2 cup crushed tomatoes"
      - "1/2 pint beef stock"
      - "1/2 Tbsp. vinegar"
      - "1/2 tsp. salt"
      - "1 tsp. brown sugar"
      - "pinch dry mustard"
      - "1/2 tsp. chili powder"
---
1. Brown ground sirloin in deep skillet, drain off excess fat, and set aside.
2. Combine remaining ingredients except cornstarch and water.
3. Simmer the mixture for 1/2 hour.
4. Make a paste with the cornstarch and water, and add to the simmering mixture, cooking about 10 minutes longer to thicken.
5. Lastly, add the browned sirloin and cook another 10 minutes.
6. Serve on buns.
