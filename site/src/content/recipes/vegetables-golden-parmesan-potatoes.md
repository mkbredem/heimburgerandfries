ingredients:
  - items:
      - "2 lbs red potatoes, cut into quarters"
      - "1/3 cup olive oil"
      - "1 1/2 tsp Italian seasoning"
      - "2 cloves garlic, minced"
      - "1 cup (4 ounces) grated Parmesan cheese"
---
1. Toss potatoes with oil, seasoning & garlic.
2. Add cheese; mix lightly.
3. Place in 15 x 10 by 1 baking pan.
4. Bake @ 400°F for 45 min.
