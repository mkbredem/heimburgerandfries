ingredients:
  - items:
      - "2 2/3 cups pretzel crumbs"
      - "2 sticks melted margarine"
      - "8 oz Cool Whip"
      - "1 cup sugar"
      - "8 oz cream cheese"
      - "2 - 10 oz pkgs frozen sliced strawberries (I use fresh when available)"
      - "1 - 16 oz can crushed pineapple"
      - "1 - large pkg. strawberry Jello"
---
1. Grease 9x13 baking pan and pat in pretzel and margarine mixture.
2. Bake at 350°F for approximately 10 minutes. Cool.
3. Next, mix softened cream cheese, sugar, and Cool Whip, and spread over pretzel crust.
4. Drain strawberries and pineapple. Add enough water to juice to make 1 1/2 cup liquid. Boil, add to jello, stirring to dissolve.
5. Add 1/2 cup ice cubes, stirring until slightly thick. Add strawberries and pineapple.
6. Pour jello mixture over cream cheese/pretzel crust.
7. Cover and refrigerate. Cut into squares and serve.
