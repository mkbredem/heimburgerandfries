ingredients:
  - items:
      - "2 cups sugar"
      - "3/4 cup light corn syrup"
      - "1/2 cup butter"
      - "2 cups whipping cream"
---
1. Bring sugar, corn syrup, butter, and 1 cup of cream slowly to a boil.
2. Gradually stir in remaining cup of whipping cream.
3. Cook (stirring constantly) until hard ball stage (254°F).
4. Pour into a buttered 8"x8" square pan that has been covered with 1/2 cup of chopped nuts.
5. When cold, cut into squares.
