      - "1 cup all-purpose flour"
      - "1 1/2 tsp. baking powder"
      - "2/3 cup sugar"
      - "1 egg"
      - "2 tsp grated orange rind"
      - "1/2 cup low-fat milk"
---
1. Preheat oven 350°F.
2. Melt 1 T of margarine in a 9 x 1 1/2 inch round cake pan over very low heat. Remove from heat.
3. Sprinkle margarine with brown sugar & arrange apple slices in overlapping pattern on top.
4. Sift together flour & baking powder.
5. Beat remaining 4 T margarine with sugar, egg, orange rind & vanilla at medium speed until smooth & creamy.
6. Add the flour mixture alternately with the milk, beating until evenly blended.
7. Pour batter over apples & spread evenly.
8. Bake 40 min., or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
9. Cool in pan on wire rack 5 min.
10. With a butter knife or a narrow metal spatula, loosen cake from sides of pan. Invert onto a plate.
11. If some of the apple topping sticks to the pan, just put the apples back where they belong & spread the sugary caramel on top with a small spatula.
12. Serve warm or at room temp., garnished with whipped topping or apple slices, if desired.

**Note:** Vanilla is mentioned in the directions but no amount is listed in the ingredients.
