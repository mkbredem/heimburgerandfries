ingredients:
  - items:
      - "5 small zucchini - sliced thinly"
      - "1 large sweet onion - sliced thinly"
      - "2 large tomatoes, peeled & chopped"
      - "4 Tbsp. margarine"
      - "16 oz shredded mozzarella cheese"
      - "1 package Ritz Crackers"
      - "4 Tbsp. melted margarine (for drizzling)"
---
1. Sauté zucchini and onion in margarine.
2. Layer the zucchini and onions in a greased 9x13 baking dish: place half the zucchini/onion mixture on the bottom, top with 1 tomato, and cover with mozzarella cheese.
3. Next repeat the same ingredients for the next layer, except place 1 package of crumbled Ritz Crackers on top of cheese.
4. Drizzle with another 4 Tbsp. melted margarine.
5. Bake at 350°F for about 1 hour.
