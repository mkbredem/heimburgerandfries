ingredients:
  - items:
      - "3/4 cup butter or margarine"
      - "3/4 cup sugar"
      - "1/4 cup molasses"
      - "3 large eggs"
      - "1 1/2 cups sifted flour"
      - "1/2 tsp. soda"
      - "3/4 tsp salt"
      - "3/4 tsp cinnamon"
      - "3/4 tsp allspice"
      - "1/4 tsp. mace"
      - "2 tsp. vanilla"
      - "2 cups diced glazed fruit"
      - "2 cups pitted dates (cut-up)"
      - "2 cups seedless raisins"
      - "1 cup chopped nuts"
      - "Brandy or sherry"
---
1. Thoroughly cream butter, and add sugar and molasses, beating until well-mixed.
2. Add eggs one at a time and beat until light and fluffy.
3. Sift flour with soda and spices. Stir in by hand.
4. Add the vanilla.
5. Make sure all fruit is soft. (If dry, steam over boiling water for a few minutes; cool.)
6. Stir in the fruit and blend mixture thoroughly.
7. Line small muffin or cupcake pans with colored foil baking cups. Spoon batter evenly in cups, filling them 3/4 full.
8. Bake at 325°F for about 50-60 minutes for muffin size, and 45 minutes for cupcake size. If cakes brown too quickly, cover loosely with a sheet of foil.
9. Leave cakes in foil cups and cool thoroughly.
10. Sprinkle with brandy or sherry. Keep tightly covered.
