ingredients:
  - group: "Shrimp"
    items:
      - "1 cup sifted flour"
      - "1/2 tsp. sugar"
      - "1/2 tsp. salt"
      - "1 slightly beaten egg"
      - "1 cup ice water"
      - "2 Tbsp. canola oil"
      - "2 pounds fresh or frozen shrimp"
  - group: "Cocktail Sauce"
    items:
      - "1 bottle Heinz Chili Sauce"
      - "prepared horseradish"
---
1. Shrimp: Mix together flour, sugar, salt, egg, ice water, and canola oil.
2. Shrimp: If shrimp is frozen, place shrimp in boiling water. Bring to a boil again and boil for 2-3 minutes. If shrimp is not frozen, they will only need to cook about 3 minutes.
3. Shrimp: Place shrimp in batter and drop into very hot oil.
4. Shrimp: Cook until shrimp is medium-brown.
5. Shrimp: Place on paper towel to drain.
6. Cocktail Sauce: Add prepared horseradish to Heinz Chili Sauce until sauce is as spicy as you like it.
