      - "1 tsp. prepared mustard"
      - "1/3 cup vinegar"
      - "1/2 tsp. salt"
      - "dash of garlic salt"
---
1. Crisply cook bacon and reserve bacon grease (drippings). Crumble cooked bacon and set aside.
2. Mix together and beat well egg, sugar, mustard, and salt.
3. Measure 1/3 cup vinegar and fill cup (2/3 cup) with water. Blend well and begin to boil.
4. Add bacon drippings and simmer for 3-5 minutes.
5. Cool [?]. Pour over leaf lettuce and toss with bacon bits.
