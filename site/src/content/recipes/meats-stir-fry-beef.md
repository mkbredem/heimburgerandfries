ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 pound Flank Steak"
      - "2 Tbsp. soy sauce"
      - "1 Tbsp. Sherry"
      - "1 tsp. sugar"
      - "3 cloves garlic - minced"
      - "1 Tbsp. salad oil"
      - "1 bunch broccoli flowerette"
      - "1 small sweet onion - sliced"
      - "1 1/2 cups sliced mushrooms"
      - "1/2 cup bean sprouts (fresh)"
      - "1 cup fresh pea pods"
      - "1 Tbsp. cornstarch"
      - "1/4 cup water"
      - "1 beef bouillon cube"
      - "1 tsp. salt"
---
1. Cut flank steak in bite-size pieces and place in a bowl with soy sauce, sherry, sugar, salt, and garlic - marinate about 2 hours.
2. Blanch broccoli about 1 minute - drain.
3. Heat oil, add beef, and cook for 5 minutes.
4. Dissolve bouillon cube in 1/4 cup water, and add 1 Tbsp. cornstarch - cook until thick.
5. Add thickened mixture to meat and cook about 1 minute.
6. Add veggies 1 at a time and cook until tender.
7. Serve over rice.
