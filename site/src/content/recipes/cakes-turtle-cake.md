ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 - German Chocolate Cake mix"
      - "4 oz caramels"
      - "1/2 cup evaporated milk"
      - "1 1/2 sticks butter"
      - "1 cup (8 oz) chocolate chips"
      - "1 cup chopped pecans"
---
1. Mix cake according to package directions.
2. Pour 1/2 of batter into 13" by 9" greased pan and bake 15 minutes at 350°F.
3. Meanwhile melt caramels in a double boiler; when melted add butter & milk.
4. Stir caramel mixture well and pour over baked cake.
5. Pour chocolate chips over caramel mixture and top with remaining batter.
6. Return pan to oven and bake for another 15 minutes.
