1. Flour and salt & pepper meat and place in small amount of canola oil in hot dutch oven.
2. Brown meat on both sides, and cover with sliced medium onion.
3. Cook over low heat for about 1 1/2 hours (covered), or until meat is tender.
4. Turn meat several times, and add water if meat does not draw its own juices.
5. When meat is tender, cut into bite-size pieces, scrape the bottom of the pan, and add enough water until pan is about 1/2 full.
6. Add 3 potatoes - diced, 2 carrots - chopped, 1 package frozen baby peas, and 1 cup sliced mushrooms.
7. Cook for about 30-45 minutes or until veggies are tender.
8. Before serving, thicken with a mixture of 3 Tbsp. flour and 1/2 cup water.
