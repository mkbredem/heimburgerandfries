ingredients:
  - items:
      - "2 pound package of frozen hash browns"
      - "8 oz. shredded cheddar cheese"
      - "1/2 cup melted margarine"
      - "1 tsp. salt"
      - "1/4 tsp. pepper"
      - "1/2 cup chopped onion"
      - "1 can cream of chicken soup"
      - "1 pt. sour cream"
  - group: "Topping"
    items:
      - "2 cups crushed cornflakes"
      - "1/4 cup melted margarine"
---
1. Mix all of the above together and place in a greased 9" x 12" casserole.
2. Top with 2 cups crushed cornflakes and 1/4 cup melted margarine.
3. Bake at 350°F for 45 minutes.
