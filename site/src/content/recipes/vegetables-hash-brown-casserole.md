ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 - 26 oz. bag frozen country-style hash browns"
      - "2 cups shredded Colby cheese"
      - "1/4 cup minced onion"
      - "1 cup milk"
      - "1/2 cup canned beef broth"
      - "2 Tbsp. melted butter"
      - "dash garlic powder"
      - "1 tsp. salt"
      - "1/4 tsp. black pepper"
---
1. Preheat the oven to 425°F.
2. Combine the frozen hash browns, cheese, and onion in a large bowl.
3. Combine the milk, beef broth, 1/2 the melted butter, the garlic powder, salt, and pepper in another bowl. Mix until well blended, then pour the mixture over the hash browns, and mix well.
4. Heat remaining butter in oven-proof pan, and cook hash browns until all of cheese has melted.
5. Bake in oven at 425°F for 45-60 min.
