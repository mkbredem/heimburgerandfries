      - "2 Tbsp. butter"
      - "2/3 cup marshmallow topping"
      - "2 cups Rice Krispies"
---
1. Melt butter in heavy pan, then add marshmallows, stirring until well-mixed.
2. Add Rice Krispies and stir until well-coated.
3. Press mixture into a 9" pie pan.
4. Freeze crust for 1 hour, then fill with favorite ice cream, topping, and refreeze until serving time.
