ingredients:
  - items:
      - "2 1/4 cups sifted flour"
      - "1/8 tsp. salt"
      - "1 tsp. almond extract"
      - "1 cup soft butter"
      - "3/4 cup crushed cornflakes"
      - "1/2 cup sugar"
      - "1 egg yolk"
      - "1 pkg. cherry chips"
---
1. Cream butter & sugar until light and fluffy.
2. Mix in egg yolk and almond extract.
3. Add sifted flour & salt; mix.
4. Stir in cornflakes and cherry chips.
5. Roll into 1" balls, place on ungreased cookie sheets, press flat with fork lengthwise and crosswise and sprinkle with sugar.
6. Bake at 375°F for 10-12 min.
7. Cool on cooling rack.
