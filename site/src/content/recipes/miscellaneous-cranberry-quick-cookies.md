ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 pkg. Pillsbury Cranberry Orange Bread Mix"
      - "3/4 cup quick cooking oats"
      - "1/2 cup coconut"
      - "1/2 cup oil"
      - "1/2 cup Craisins (Dried Cranberries)"
      - "1/2 tsp. grated orange peel"
      - "1 Tbsp. water"
      - "2 eggs"
---
1. Heat oven to 350°F.
2. In large bowl, combine bread mix, oats, cranberries, coconut, and orange peel; mix well.
3. Add oil, water, and eggs; mix well.
4. Drop by heaping teaspoonfuls 2" apart onto ungreased cookie sheets.
5. Bake at 350°F for 10-13 minutes or until bottoms are golden brown.
