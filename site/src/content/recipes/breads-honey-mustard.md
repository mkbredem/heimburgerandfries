ingredients:
  - items:
      - "3/4 cup sour cream"
      - "1/3 cup mayo"
      - "1/2 cup prepared mustard"
      - "1/3 cup honey"
      - "1 Tbsp. lemon juice"
      - "Salt & pepper to taste"
---
1. Mix mayo, sour cream & mustard with wire whisk.
2. Add honey, lemon juice, salt & pepper, stirring until well combined.
