ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 box Oyster Crackers"
      - "1 cup Puritan Oil (heated)"
      - "1 package Hidden Valley Ranch Dressing"
      - "2 tsp. garlic powder"
      - "1/2 tsp. lemon pepper"
      - "1/2 tsp. dill weed"
---
1. Toss crackers with heated oil.
2. Blend all dry ingredients and sprinkle over oiled crackers.
3. Let sit for 24 hours, then store in a container.
