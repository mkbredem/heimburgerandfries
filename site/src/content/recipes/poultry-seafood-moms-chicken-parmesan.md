      - "fresh shredded Parmesan cheese"
---
1. Place chicken in baking pan and cover with spaghetti sauce.
2. Cover sauce with mozzarella cheese and top with fresh Parmesan.
3. Bake covered at 350°F for 1 hour.
4. Uncover and cook an additional 15 minutes.
