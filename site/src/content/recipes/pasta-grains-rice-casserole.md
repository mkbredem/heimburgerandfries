ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 1/2 cups instant rice (uncooked)"
      - "1 can Campbell's French Onion Soup"
      - "1 can Campbell's Beef Broth"
      - "16 oz can mushroom stems & pieces"
      - "1 stick margarine, sliced"
---
1. Mix all ingredients together and place in a large greased casserole.
2. Bake covered for 45 minutes at 350°F.
3. Uncover and cook for 15 minutes longer.
