ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1/2 cup peanut butter"
      - "1/2 cup Crisco"
      - "1/2 cup brown sugar"
      - "1/2 cup white sugar"
      - "1 tsp. vanilla"
      - "1 egg"
      - "1 1/2 cups flour"
      - "1 tsp. soda"
      - "1/4 tsp. salt"
---
1. Cream Crisco and sugars.
2. Add egg, vanilla, and peanut butter. Blend well.
3. Mix in sifted flour, soda, and salt.
4. Shape into balls, roll in sugar, and press criss-cross marks with a fork.
5. Bake at 375°F for 10-15 minutes.
