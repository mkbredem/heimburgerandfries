      - "1 package tortillas (heated)"
      - "1/2 lb. bacon cooked crisply & crumbled"
      - "1 dozen eggs - well beaten"
      - "3-4 Tbsp. margarine"
      - "3-4 Idaho potatoes boiled (about 20 minutes) and grated"
      - "margarine (enough to cover the bottom of the skillet)"
      - "1/2 pound colby cheese, shredded"
      - "salsa"
---
1. Melt margarine in skillet, add beaten eggs, crumbled bacon, and scramble.
2. Next, boil 3-4 Idaho potatoes (about 20 minutes) and grate.
3. Use enough margarine to cover the bottom of the skillet, and fry the potatoes.
4. Shred 1/2 pound colby cheese.
5. Place eggs on tortillas, cover with fried potatoes, add shredded cheese, and top with salsa.
