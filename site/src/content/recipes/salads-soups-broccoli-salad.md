ingredients:
  - group: "Salad"
    items:
      - "1 bunch broccoli flowerettes"
      - "8 slices of bacon (cubed & fried crisply)"
      - "4-5 green onions (diced finely)"
      - "1/2 cup raisins (optional)"
      - "1/2 cup walnuts - chopped"
  - group: "Dressing"
    items:
      - "1 cup mayonnaise"
      - "1/2 cup sugar"
      - "3 Tbsp. vinegar"
---
1. Combine broccoli, bacon, green onions, raisins, and walnuts.
2. Mix together mayonnaise, sugar, and vinegar.
3. Pour over broccoli mixture, chill, and serve.
