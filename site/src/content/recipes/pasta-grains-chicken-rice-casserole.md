  - group: "Gravy"
    items:
      - "10 Tbsp. flour"
      - "2 cups chicken broth"
---
1. Cook chicken as directed for chicken and noodles.
2. Place 1 1/2 cups Uncle Ben's Rice in large greased casserole. Add 3 cups of chicken broth, and mix rice and broth together.
3. Cover and cook on high in microwave for 6 minutes. Then cook rice on Cook Power 5 for about 12 minutes.
4. Then add 2 cans of cream of mushroom soup, 16 oz sliced mushrooms, chicken, and enough broth to make mixture moist.
5. Cook for 1 hour at 350°F.
6. Gravy: Place flour in saucepan and gradually add chicken broth. Cook over low heat until thickened. Serve over casserole if desired.
