ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 pound ground sirloin"
      - "1 can chicken gumbo soup"
      - "1/4 cup water"
      - "1/4 cup catsup"
      - "2 tsp dry mustard"
      - "1/2 tsp salt"
      - "1 medium onion chopped finely"
---
1. Brown ground sirloin with chopped onion - drain excess fat.
2. Add remaining ingredients and simmer approximately 1 hour.
