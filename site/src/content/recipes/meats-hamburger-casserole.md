1. Layer the following ingredients in greased 2 qt. casserole: thinly sliced potatoes, peas, chopped celery, chopped carrots, chopped onions.
2. Place browned meat on top.
3. Empty can of tomato juice into a bowl.
4. Place pea juice into soup can and fill the can the rest of the way with water.
5. Mix juice with soup and pour over the top of the meat.
6. Bake at 350°F for 1 1/2 hours. Cover for the first hour, then uncover.
