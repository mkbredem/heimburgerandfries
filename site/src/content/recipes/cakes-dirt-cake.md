ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 1/2 bags Oreos"
      - "2 small pkgs. vanilla instant pudding"
      - "3 1/2 cup milk"
      - "1 - 8 oz. pkg. cream cheese"
      - "1 - 12 oz. container Cool Whip"
      - "1/2 - 1 cup powdered sugar"
      - "gummie worms"
      - "flower pot or small pail"
      - "silk flower"
---
1. Crush Oreos in food processor.
2. Mix other ingredients.
3. Line bottom of flower pot with foil.
4. Layer cookies, pudding, etc., making sure to end with cookie crumbs.
5. Place a silk flower in pot, and place gummie worms as though they are coming out of dirt onto the side of the pot.

**Note:** Makes a nice centerpiece.
