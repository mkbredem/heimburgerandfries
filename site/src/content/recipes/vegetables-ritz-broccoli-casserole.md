ingredients:
  - items:
      - "2 bunches of broccoli flowerettes"
      - "1 cup diced American cheese"
      - "2 eggs (well-beaten)"
      - "1/2 cup mayonnaise (Miracle Whip)"
      - "1/2 can mushroom soup - undiluted"
      - "Ritz Crackers"
      - "butter"
---
1. Steam broccoli for about 15 minutes - set aside.
2. In a bowl, beat eggs, add soup, cheese, and mayo - mix.
3. Pour over broccoli.
4. Crumble Ritz Crackers over top - dot with butter.
5. Place in a 9x13 baking dish (greased) and bake for 25-30 min. at 350°F.
