ingredients:
  - items:
      - "18 oz Tang"
      - "1 cup sugar (I don't use the sugar)"
      - "1 package pre-sweetened lemon Kool-Aid"
      - "1 - 3 oz. Apricot Jello"
      - "2 1/2 tsp. ground cinnamon"
      - "1 tsp ground cloves"
      - "1/2 cup decaf. instant tea mix"
---
1. Mix all ingredients and store in air-tight container.
2. Place about 1 1/2 Tbsp. mix per 1 cup of boiling liquid (H2O).

**Note:** Sugar is optional: "I don't use the sugar."
