1. Use as many boneless, skinless chicken breasts as you need.
2. Place in a baking pan.
3. Slice onion over chicken.
4. Then cut up tomatoes over onions.
5. Cover mixture with Italian Dressing.
6. Bake at 350°F (covered) for 1 hour.
7. Uncover and continue cooking for another 15 minutes.
