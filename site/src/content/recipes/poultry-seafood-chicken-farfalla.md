ingredients:
  - items:
      - "6 oz. uncooked farfalla"
      - "2 tsp. margarine"
      - "1 cup chopped sweet onion"
      - "2 cups sliced mushrooms"
      - "2 cups chicken broth"
      - "2 Tbsp. plus 1 tsp. cornstarch"
      - "3/4 tsp. salt"
      - "1/4 tsp. pepper"
      - "2 cups cooked chicken breasts (torn in bite-size pieces)"
      - "1/2 cup frozen baby peas"
      - "2 Tbsp. shredded Parmesan cheese"
---
1. Cook farfalla per package directions.
2. Heat margarine in large skillet, and saute onion & margarine until slightly brown.
3. Remove mushrooms & onions from pan.
4. Add chicken broth to pan, and slowly add cornstarch, cooking over medium heat until slightly thickened.
5. Season sauce with salt & pepper, and add pasta, vegetables, chicken, & peas - mix.
6. Place mixture into greased casserole.
7. Sprinkle with Parmesan cheese and bake at 350°F for 25-30 minutes.
