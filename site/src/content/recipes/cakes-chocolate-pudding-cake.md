ingredients:
  - items:
      - "2 squares semi-sweet chocolate"
      - "1/2 cup sugar"
      - "1/2 cup water"
      - "4 egg yolks"
      - "1 envelope Knox gelatin"
      - "2 Tbsp. water"
      - "1/2 pint whipped cream"
      - "4 egg whites, stiffly beaten"
      - "1 large angel food cake"
      - "Crushed nuts (optional)"
---
1. Melt chocolate, sugar, and water in double boiler.
2. Beat 4 egg yolks. Add to above ingredients.
3. Soften 1 envelope Knox gelatin in 2 Tbsp. water and add to above ingredients; cool all ingredients.
4. Add 1/2 pint whipped cream and 4 egg whites stiffly beaten. Fold into cooled mixture.
5. Next, tear a large angel food cake into small pieces. Place in a 9"x11" cake pan.
6. Alternate layers - cake, pudding, cake, pudding.
7. May place crushed nuts on top if desired.
8. Refrigerate.
