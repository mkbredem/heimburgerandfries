ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 1/2 cup sugar"
      - "2 eggs"
      - "1 cup buttermilk"
      - "2 1/4 cup cake flour (or plain flour)"
      - "2 - 1 oz bottles red food coloring, or 1 & 1 bottle H2O"
      - "3 T Nestle's sweet milk cocoa"
      - "1/2 cup Crisco"
      - "1/4 tsp salt"
      - "1 tsp vanilla"
      - "1 tsp soda"
      - "1 tsp apple cider vinegar"
---
1. Mix coloring & cocoa together in small bowl.
2. Cream Crisco & sugar.
3. Add eggs & coloring & beat well.
4. Add buttermilk, vanilla, salt & flour. Beat well with mixer.
5. Mix soda & vinegar together, then add to mixture & beat hard.
6. Bake in 2 layers. Bake 30 min @ 350°F.
