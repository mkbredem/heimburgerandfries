1. Dumplings: Mix like you would biscuit dough.
2. Dumplings: Roll out - cover dough with Jonathan apples that have been peeled, cored, and sliced 1/2" thick.
3. Dumplings: Sprinkle sugar and cinnamon over apples and roll up jellyroll style.
4. Dumplings: Pour syrup over roll and bake at 350°F for 1 hour.
5. Syrup: Mix together and boil for 3 minutes.
