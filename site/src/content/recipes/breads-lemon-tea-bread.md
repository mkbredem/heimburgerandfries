ingredients:
  - group: Bread
    items:
      - 3/4 cup margarine
      - 2 cups sugar
      - Grated rind of 2 lemons
      - 4 eggs
      - 3 cups flour
      - 2 tsp baking powder
      - 1/2 tsp salt
      - 1 cup milk
  - group: Glaze
    items:
      - Juice of 2 lemons
      - 1/2 cup sugar
---
1. Cream the margarine and 2 cups of sugar with the lemon rind.
2. Add the eggs and beat well.
3. Add the dry ingredients, alternating with the milk.
4. Squeeze about 1 Tbsp of lemon juice into the batter for better flavor.
5. Bake in greased loaf pans at 325°F for 1 hour.
6. Mix 1/2 cup sugar with the remaining lemon juice. Pour over the bread while hot, then cool completely on a wire rack.
