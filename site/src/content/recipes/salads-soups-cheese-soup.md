ingredients:
  - items:
      - "4 cups chicken broth"
      - "1 cup chopped sweet onion"
      - "1 cup shredded carrots"
      - "2 celery stalks (cook whole & remove before adding the cheese)"
      - "1 pound Velveeta cheese"
      - "3 Idaho potatoes cubed"
---
1. Cook together chicken broth, onion, carrots, and celery for about 20 minutes.
2. Remove celery, add cheese.
3. Simmer until thoroughly heated (Do Not Boil).
