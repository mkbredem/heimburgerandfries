ingredients:
  - group: "Cookies"
    items:
      - "1 1/2 cups powdered sugar"
      - "1 cup butter"
      - "1 egg"
      - "1/2 tsp. almond extract"
      - "1 tsp. baking soda"
      - "1 tsp. vanilla"
      - "2 1/2 cups flour"
      - "1 tsp. cream of tartar"
  - group: "Icing"
    items:
      - "4 cups sifted powdered sugar"
      - "3/4 stick butter"
      - "3/4 tsp. vanilla"
      - "2-3 Tbsp. milk"
---
1. Mix powdered sugar, butter, vanilla, almond extract, and egg in large bowl until well blended.
2. Stir in flour, baking soda, and cream of tartar.
3. Cover and chill for 2-3 hours.
4. Heat oven to 375°F.
5. Divide dough into halves. Roll each half 3/16" thick on lightly floured cloth-covered board.
6. Cut into desired shapes with 3" cutters.
7. Place on ungreased cookie sheet and bake 7-10 minutes.
8. Cool on a wire rack.
