ingredients:
  - items:
      - "8 oz cream cheese"
      - "8 oz Miracle Whip"
      - "8 oz sour cream"
      - "8 oz Avocado Dip"
      - "Worcestershire sauce"
      - "sweet onion chopped finely"
      - "1 pound shredded Colby cheese"
      - "shredded lettuce"
      - "tomatoes - chopped"
---
1. Blend cream cheese, Miracle Whip, sour cream, and Avocado Dip.
2. Add a dash of Worcestershire sauce and mix well.
3. Spread mixture on a large plate.
4. Top with chopped onion, lettuce (shredded), tomatoes, and shredded cheese.
5. Cover, chill and serve with tortilla chips.
