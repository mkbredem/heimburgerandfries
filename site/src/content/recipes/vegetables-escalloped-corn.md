ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 1/4 cups finely crushed Ritz Crackers"
      - "1/2 cup margarine"
      - "2 Tbsp. butter"
      - "1 1/4 cups milk"
      - "1 - 10 oz. package frozen whole kernel corn"
      - "1/2 tsp. salt"
      - "2 Tbsp. flour"
      - "1/2 tsp. onion salt"
      - "2 eggs, beaten"
---
1. Combine first 2 ingredients & save 1/2 cup for topping.
2. Line 9" pie pan or 8" square baking dish with remainder of crumbs.
3. Combine butter, 1 cup of milk, corn, & salt in saucepan. Heat until boiling.
4. Reduce heat and cook 3 minutes.
5. Mix flour and remaining 1/4 cup milk into a smooth paste, and slowly add to hot mixture, stirring constantly. Cook until thick.
6. Add remaining ingredients and cook slightly.
7. Pour into crumb-lined pie pan or baking dish. Top with remaining crumbs.
8. Bake for 20-30 minutes at 375°F.
