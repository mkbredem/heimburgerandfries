  - items:
      - "1 box Rice Chex"
      - "12 oz. package chocolate chips"
      - "12 oz. bag peanut butter chips"
      - "1 pound box of powdered sugar"
---
1. Melt chips together and pour over Rice Chex.
2. Place Chex into large container, pour powdered sugar over mixture, put lid on, and shake until mixture is well-coated.
