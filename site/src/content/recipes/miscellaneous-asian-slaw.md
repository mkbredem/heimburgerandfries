ingredients:
  - items:
      - "2 (3 oz) packages beef-flavored ramen noodle soup mix"
      - "2 (8.5 oz) packages slaw mix"
      - "1 cup sliced almonds"
      - "1 cup sunflower kernels"
      - "1 bunch green onions, chopped"
      - "1/2 cup sugar"
      - "3/4 cup oil (1/2 of it is sesame oil)"
      - "1/3 cup white vinegar"
---
1. Remove flavor packets from soup mix, & set aside; crush noodles.
2. Place noodles in bottom of a large bowl.
3. Top with slaw mix; sprinkle with almonds, sunflower kernels, & green onions.
4. Whisk together contents from flavor packets, sugar, oil, & vinegar; pour over slaw.
5. Cover & chill 24 hours.
6. Toss before serving.
