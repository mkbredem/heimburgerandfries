ingredients:
  - items:
      - "2 lbs. ground raw peanuts"
      - "2 lbs. powdered sugar"
      - "6 eggs"
      - "2 Tbsp. cinnamon"
      - "pinch of salt"
---
1. Beat eggs & sugar for 30 minutes.
2. Add 2 Tbsp. cinnamon, a pinch of salt, and peanuts.
3. Chill in refrig. overnight.
4. Roll out on powdered sugar board.
5. Cut in star shapes and bake on ungreased cookie sheets for 15 min. at 350°F.
