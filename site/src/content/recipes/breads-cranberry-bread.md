ingredients:
  - items:
      - "2 cups flour"
      - "1 cup sugar"
      - "1/2 tsp. baking powder"
      - "1/2 tsp. baking soda"
      - "1/2 tsp. salt"
      - "1 well-beaten egg"
      - "1 cup fresh cranberries (sliced lengthwise)"
      - "1 cup chopped nuts"
      - "3 Tbsp. soft butter"
      - "juice of one orange plus grated orange rind (add water to orange juice to make 3/4 cup), boil"
---
1. Sift dry ingredients, and add orange juice, rind, water liquid.
2. Add egg and butter and beat well.
3. Add raw cranberries and nuts by hand.
4. Bake in greased loaf pan for one hour at 350°F.
5. Let stand for 24 hours before cutting.
