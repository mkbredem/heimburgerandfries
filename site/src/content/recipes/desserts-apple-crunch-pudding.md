ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1/2 cup white sugar & brown sugar"
      - "2 Tbsp. flour"
      - "1 tsp. cinnamon"
      - "1/2 tsp. of allspice & nutmeg"
      - "2 cups crushed cornflakes"
      - "1/2 cup butter"
      - "4 cups apples"
      - "1/2 cup chopped pecans"
---
1. Mix sugar, flour, & spices together, and stir in cornflakes.
2. Next, add melted butter.
3. Place half mixture into a shallow baking dish, top with apples, cover with remaining mixture, and top with nuts.
4. Bake at 350°F for 25 minutes.
