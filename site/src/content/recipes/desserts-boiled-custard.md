ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 gallon whole milk"
      - "10 whole eggs"
      - "2 1/2 cups sugar"
      - "2 tsp. vanilla"
---
1. Beat eggs until well-beaten and add to milk & sugar; place in a large pan.
2. Place this pan into a larger pan containing water (water should go 1/2 way up the outside of the pan).
3. Cook over medium heat for 1 hour, stirring 3-4 times.
4. When cool, add 2 tsp. vanilla.
5. Strain and chill in a large container.
