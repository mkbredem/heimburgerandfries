ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 - 24 oz. low fat cottage cheese"
      - "1/2 cup finely chopped broccoli"
      - "1/2 cup finely chopped carrot"
      - "2 Tbsp. finely chopped green onion"
      - "2 Tbsp. finely chopped fresh parsley"
      - "1 envelope Good Seasons Italian Salad Dressing Mix"
---
1. Place cottage cheese in blender or food processor, cover. Blend on low speed until pureed.
2. Stir in remaining ingredients. Refrigerate.
3. Serve with assorted cut-up vegetables. Garnish as desired.

**Note:** Makes 4 cups.
