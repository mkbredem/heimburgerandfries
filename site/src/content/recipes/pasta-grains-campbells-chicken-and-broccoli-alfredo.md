ingredients:
  - items:
      - "8 oz linguine"
      - "1 cup broccoli"
      - "2 Tbsp butter or olive oil"
      - "1 lb chicken (boneless, skinless), cubed"
      - "1 can 10 3/4 oz Cream of Mushroom 98% fat free soup"
      - "1/2 cup milk"
      - "1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese"
      - "1/4 tsp. pepper"
---
1. Cook pasta according to package directions. Add broccoli for last 4 minutes of cooking time. Drain.
2. Heat butter or olive oil in skillet. Add chicken and cook until browned, stirring often.
3. Add soup, milk, cheese, pepper and pasta. Heat through.
4. Add additional Parmesan cheese and serve.
