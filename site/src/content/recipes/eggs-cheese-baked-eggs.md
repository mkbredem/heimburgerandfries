ingredients:
  - items:
      - "10 slices bread - cubed (crusts removed)"
      - "1/2 pound Velveeta"
      - "1/2 cup butter"
      - "6 eggs"
      - "2 cups milk"
---
1. Spread cubed bread evenly on bottom of 9"x13" greased Pyrex dish.
2. Melt Velveeta and butter together and pour over bread.
3. Blend eggs and milk together and pour over first 2 ingredients.
4. Refrigerate overnight.
5. Cook (bake) at 350°F for 1 hour - 1 hour 15 minutes.
