1. Sponge Cake: Beat for 10 minutes.
2. Sponge Cake: Bake for about 30 minutes. Put in 9"x12" pan in a 275°F oven and then gradually increase oven temp to 350°F.
3. Sponge Cake: Cool, and cut in squares.
4. Ice and roll in crushed peanuts.

**Note:** No flour is listed for the sponge cake on the page.
