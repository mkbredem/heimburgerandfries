      - "5 Tbsp. flour"
      - "1 cup of milk"
      - "approximately 1/2 pound Velveeta cheese, cut up in chunks"
---
1. Sauce: Place 5 Tbsp. flour in saucepan. Gradually add 1 cup of milk, stirring until smooth.
2. Sauce: Cook in saucepan until mixture thickens.
3. Sauce: Then add approximately 1/2 pound Velveeta cheese cut up in chunks and stir constantly until sauce is creamy. (May add more cheese if the mixture is not cheesy enough. Add more milk if the mixture is too cheesy.)
4. Pour sauce over cauliflower and serve.
