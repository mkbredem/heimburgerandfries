ingredients:
  - items:
      - "12 macaroons - crushed"
      - "1/2 cup firmly packed brown sugar"
      - "1 fresh pineapple"
      - "1 pint sour cream"
---
1. Mix together macaroons, sugar, and sour cream.
2. Chill for several hours to soften the crumbs. Do not stir again. (The macaroons will break into small crumbs.)
3. Cut off the top of the pineapple 1" below the leaves and hollow it out.
4. Place the dip into the pineapple, and replace the top.
