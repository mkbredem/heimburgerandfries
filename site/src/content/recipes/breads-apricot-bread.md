ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 cup dried apricots"
      - "1/2 cup sugar"
      - "2 Tbsp. butter"
      - "1 egg"
      - "1/4 cup water"
      - "1/2 cup orange juice"
      - "2 cups flour"
      - "2 tsp. baking powder"
      - "1/4 tsp. soda"
      - "1 tsp. salt"
      - "1/2 cup nuts"
---
1. Cream together butter and sugar.
2. Add egg, water, and orange juice.
3. Then add sifted dry ingredients (flour, baking powder, soda, and salt).
4. Lastly, fold in apricots and nuts, and pour into a greased, floured bread pan.
5. Bake for about 1 hour at 350°F.
