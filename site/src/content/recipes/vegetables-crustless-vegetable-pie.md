ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 small eggplant (peeled & cubed)"
      - "2 medium zucchini (cubed)"
      - "1 medium onion, chopped finely"
      - "1/4 cup oil"
      - "4 medium tomatoes, peeled & chopped OR 1 - 1 pound can whole tomatoes, chopped & drained"
      - "3 eggs"
      - "3/4 cup Parmesan cheese"
      - "1 Tbsp. parsley"
      - "1/2 tsp. basil"
      - "1/2 tsp. oregano"
      - "1/4 pound shredded mozzarella cheese"
      - "salt & pepper"
---
1. Sauté eggplant, zucchini, and onion in oil until vegetables are softened (about 5-10 minutes).
2. Add tomatoes, cover and simmer 20-25 minutes until mixture is soft. (If you use canned tomatoes reduce cooking time to 10 minutes.)
3. Transfer to bowl & cool.
4. Preheat oven to 350°F.
5. Beat eggs with cheese, parsley, basil, & oregano & add to vegetables.
6. Pour mixture into a greased 10" pie pan & top with 1/4 c. cheese.
7. Layer remaining vegetables & cheese. Top with mozzarella.
8. Bake for 40-45 minutes at 350°F.
