ingredients:
  - group: "Cake"
    items:
      - "1 box Duncan Hines Lemon Supreme Cake Mix"
      - "1 pkg. lemon instant pudding mix"
      - "4 eggs"
      - "1/2 cup water"
      - "1/2 cup key lime juice"
      - "1/2 cup vegetable oil"
  - group: "Icing"
    items:
      - "2 cups powdered sugar"
      - "1/4 cup key lime juice"
---
1. Cake: Combine ingredients and beat 2 minutes at medium speed with electric mixer.
2. Cake: Pour batter into greased 13x9x2 pan.
3. Cake: Bake at 350°F for 35 minutes or until toothpick comes clean.
4. Cake: Let pan cool on wire rack.
5. Icing: Combine powdered sugar and key lime juice. Drizzle or spread over cake.
