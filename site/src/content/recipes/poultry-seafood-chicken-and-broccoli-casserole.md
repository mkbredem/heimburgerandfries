  - items:
      - "4 large chicken breasts (skins & ribs)"
      - "1 large sweet onion"
      - "salt & pepper"
      - "2 bunches of broccoli flowerettes"
      - "1/2 cup of flour"
      - "3 cups of chicken broth"
      - "2 cups of fresh or canned mushrooms"
      - "2 2/3 cups crushed cornflakes"
      - "3 Tbsp. melted margarine"
---
1. Cook chicken breasts in a large pan; cover with water, slice onion in water, and add salt & pepper. Cook about 1 1/2 hour.
2. When tender, debone chicken and set aside.
3. Steam 2 bunches of broccoli flowerettes and set aside.
4. Place 1/2 cup of flour in a saucepan, and slowly add 3 cups of chicken broth, stirring constantly. Cook over medium heat until mixture thickens.
5. Cover the bottom of 9" x 13" greased pan with approx. 1 cup of thickened chicken broth.
6. Place cooked broccoli over layer of sauce, and top with 2 cups of fresh or canned mushrooms. Place chicken on top of mushrooms.
7. Cover chicken with remaining thickened broth. Top with 2 2/3 cups crushed cornflakes.
8. Drizzle 3 Tbsp. melted margarine over cornflakes.
9. Bake at 350°F for 45 minutes - 1 hour.
