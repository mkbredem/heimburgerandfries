      - "3 eggs"
      - "2 Tbsp. parsley flakes"
      - "salt & pepper to taste"
      - "15 oz. spaghetti sauce"
      - "1 16 oz pkg. manicotti"
---
1. Grease a 9"x13" baking dish. Pour in enough spaghetti sauce to cover bottom of baking dish.
2. Cook manicotti according to package directions. (Pour cold water over shells and leave them in water until you stuff them.)
3. In a bowl mix ricotta cheese, 3 eggs, parsley, and shredded mozzarella (all but about 1/2 cup).
4. Stuff shells, place in pan over sauce, cover with remaining sauce, and top with remaining mozzarella cheese.
5. Cover pan with foil and bake at 350°F for 30 minutes.
