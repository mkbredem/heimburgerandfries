ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1/2 cup uncooked rice"
      - "1/2 cup water"
      - "1/2 tsp. salt"
      - "2 3/4 cup milk"
      - "1/2 cup seedless raisins"
      - "3 Tbsp. butter"
      - "2 beaten eggs"
      - "1/3 cup sugar"
      - "1/2 tsp. vanilla"
---
1. Combine rice, water, & salt. Bring to a boil.
2. Reduce heat, cover, & cook about 9 minutes.
3. Stir in 2 1/4 cup milk, cover, & cook over low heat for 1 hour, stirring occasionally.
4. (Meanwhile, cover raisins with boiling water and set aside.)
5. Add butter to hot pudding mixture, stirring constantly.
6. Cook about 5 minutes or until mixture thickens.
7. Combine eggs, sugar, and remaining milk and add to pudding mixture.
8. Remove from heat, add drained raisins & vanilla.
9. Chill.
