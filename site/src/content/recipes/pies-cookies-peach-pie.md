ingredients:
  - group: "Filling"
    items:
      - "1 cup sugar"
      - "3-4 Tbsp. cornstarch"
      - "2 Tbsp. light corn syrup"
      - "1 cup water"
      - "Peach Jell-O"
---
1. Bring to a boil sugar, corn syrup, cornstarch, and water.
2. Boil for 5 minutes, then add 2 Tbsp. peach Jell-O, and cool.
3. Add 1 tsp. almond extract, mix, and fill a pie crust.
4. Bake at 350°F for 45 min to 1 hour.
