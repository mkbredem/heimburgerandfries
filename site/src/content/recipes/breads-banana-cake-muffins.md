ingredients:
  - items:
      - "3 cup sifted flour"
      - "2 tsp baking soda"
      - "1/2 tsp salt"
      - "1 cup shortening (Crisco)"
      - "2 cup sugar"
      - "3/4 cup milk"
      - "2 eggs (slightly beaten)"
      - "5 bananas (extra soft)"
---
1. Keep sugar separate.
2. In separate dish add milk.
3. In separate dish add slightly beaten egg.
4. In separate dish add dry, sifted ingredients (salt, soda, flour).
5. In separate dish add 5 bananas.
6. Muffins: Mash bananas with potato masher. Set aside.
7. Muffins: Spray Pam in muffin pan.
8. Muffins: Cream together sugar and Crisco until fluffy.
9. Muffins: Add 2 Tbsp of milk, then eggs to creamed mixture.
10. Muffins: Alternately add dried ingredients and milk and beat until mixed.
11. Muffins: Bake 350°F for about 30 mins.
12. Cake: Cut out parchment paper and line the bottom of 3 8 or 9 inch cake pans.
13. Cake: Add mixture. Bake 350°F for about 30 mins.
