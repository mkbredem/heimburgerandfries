      - "1/4 tsp. sugar"
      - "2 tsp. cinnamon"
---
1. Mix shortening, sugar, and eggs thoroughly.
2. Blend flour, cream of tartar, soda, and salt. Stir into shortening mixture.
3. Shape dough into 1" balls. Roll in mixture of 2 Tbsp. sugar and cinnamon.
4. Place 2 inches apart on ungreased baking sheet.
5. Bake 8-10 min. at 400°F.
