ingredients:
  - items:
      - "2 pounds veal - cubed"
      - "3/4 cup flour - mixed with 1 tsp. salt & 1/4 tsp. pepper"
      - "1/4 cup oil"
      - "4 oz can mushrooms - drained"
      - "2 small onions - thinly sliced"
      - "1 1/2 tsp. sugar"
      - "1/2 tsp. oregano"
      - "2 small cloves garlic minced"
      - "1 - 15 oz. can whole tomatoes"
---
1. Roll veal cubes in seasoned flour and fry in hot oil until well-browned.
2. Place in a crockpot.
3. Add all the remaining ingredients. Mix well.
4. Cover and cook on high for 1 hour, then reduce heat and cook for 6-8 hours on low.
5. Serve over fettucini.
