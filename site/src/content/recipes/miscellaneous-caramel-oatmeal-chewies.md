      - "1/4 tsp. salt"

      - "1 cup chocolate chips"
      - "1/4 cup water"
      - "1 - 14 oz bag caramels"

1. Combine oats, flour, brown sugar, soda, & salt, and add margarine, mixing until mixture is crumbly (reserve 1 cup for topping).
2. Press remaining mixture into a greased 13x9" pan.
3. Bake at 350°F for 10 min.
4. Cool & top with nuts & chocolate pieces.
5. In small pan melt caramels & water over low heat. Stir until smooth, drizzle over choc. & nuts to within 1/4" of pan edges.
6. Sprinkle with reserved topping.
7. Bake 15-18 minutes until brown.
8. Chill until set, and cut into bars.
9. Store tightly covered.
