ingredients:
  - items:
      - "3 cups sugar"
      - "1 cup margarine"
      - "2/3 cup evaporated milk"
      - "7 oz. jar Kraft Marshmallow Creme"
      - "1 tsp vanilla"
      - "12 oz pkg. chocolate chips"
---
1. Combine sugar, margarine, and milk in a heavy 2 1/2 qt. saucepan, and bring to a full rolling boil, stirring constantly.
2. Continue boiling another 5 minutes stirring constantly.
3. Remove from heat and stir in chocolate chips until melted.
4. Add marshmallow creme and vanilla, stirring until well-blended.
5. Pour into a greased 13"x9" pan.
6. Cool at room temperature, and cut into squares.
