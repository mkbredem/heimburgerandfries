ingredients:
  - items:
      - "2 - 11 1/2 oz pkgs. milk chocolate chips"
      - "8 oz heavy whipping cream"
  - group: "Optional"
    items:
      - "1/4 tsp. mint extract"
      - "1 - 2 Tbsp. Creme de Menthe"
---
1. In a fondue pot, melt chips and add whipping cream. (Add optional ingredients if desired.)
2. Stir until smooth.

**Note:** Use fresh strawberries, pineapple, cheese cake, angel food cake, marshmallows.
