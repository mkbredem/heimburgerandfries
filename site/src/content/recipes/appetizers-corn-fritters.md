ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 1/2 cup flour"
      - "1 1/4 cups frozen corn"
      - "1 1/2 cups (corn milk)"
      - "3 tsp baking powder"
      - "3/4 tsp salt"
      - "1 beaten egg"
---
1. Place corn in microwave covered with water. Cook approx. 7 min.
2. Drain liquid into measuring cup - add milk until you have 1 1/2 cups liquid.
3. Add remaining dry ingredients.
4. Drop by tsp. into 375°F oil. Fry 3-4 min.
5. Drain and sprinkle with powdered sugar.
