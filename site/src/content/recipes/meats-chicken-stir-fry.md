ingredients:
  - items:
      - "4 boneless, skinless chicken breast halves"
      - "1 Tbsp. canola oil"
      - "2 cups sliced mushrooms"
      - "2 tsp. lemon juice"
      - "1 tsp. sesame seeds"
      - "4 Tbsp. soy sauce"
      - "2 Tbsp. butter"
---
1. Clean chicken, and cut into bite-size pieces.
2. In large frying pan, spread 1 Tbsp. of oil and saute sliced chicken.
3. Add 1 Tbsp. of soy sauce, 1 Tbsp. butter, and a dash of salt and pepper.
4. When the chicken appears white on all sides, add 2 tsp. lemon juice, and push chicken to 1 side of the pan.
5. Add mushrooms to the other side of the pan, pour 1 Tbsp. soy sauce over the mushroom, then add 1 Tbsp. butter.
6. After 8-10 minutes, sprinkle 1 tsp. of sesame seeds over the chicken.
