ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 cup Crisco"
      - "1 cup brown sugar"
      - "1 cup white sugar"
      - "2 beaten eggs"
      - "1 tsp. vanilla"
      - "1 1/2 cup flour"
      - "1/2 cup chopped black walnuts"
      - "1 tsp. salt"
      - "1 tsp. baking soda"
      - "3 cups oatmeal"
---
1. Cream Crisco and sugars.
2. Add eggs and vanilla and beat well.
3. Add sifted dry ingredients.
4. Next, add oatmeal and nut meats.
5. Shape in rolls and wrap in waxed paper.
6. Chill thoroughly, and slice 1/4" thick and bake on ungreased cookie sheet at 350°F for about 10 minutes.
