  - items:
      - "1 pound bacon (diced and browned)"
      - "5 pounds potatoes (peeled & diced)"
      - "2-3 sweet onions (diced)"
      - "dash of lemon pepper"
      - "seasoned salt to taste"
      - "1 Tbsp. flour"
      - "shredded cheddar cheese"
      - "water"
      - "milk"
---
1. Brown and drain the bacon.
2. Add half of the diced potatoes and brown.
3. Drain and add diced onions and bacon.
4. Add remaining potatoes & cover with water.
5. Season with seasoned salt and a little lemon pepper.
6. Cook for 2-3 hours until liquid is cooked down and then add milk.
7. Cook until hot.
8. Add 1 Tbsp. flour to thicken.
9. Serve with shredded cheddar cheese on top.
