ingredients:
  - items:
      - "5 pieces boneless, skinless chicken breast"
      - "2 cans Cream of Chicken Soup"
      - "1 package squiggly noodles - cooked"
      - "2 Tbsp. canola oil"
      - "1 can of water"
---
1. Place about 2 Tbsp. canola oil in skillet.
2. Brown chicken in heated oil.
3. Remove chicken from skillet (pour remaining canola oil out).
4. Place 2 cans of cream of chicken soup into the skillet, and add 1 can of water.
5. Stir until smooth and add cooked chicken.
6. Simmer about 1 1/2 hours (covered).
7. Place over cooked squiggly noodles.
