1. Strawberry Jam: Pour into prepared containers, leaving 1/2 inch space at top for expansion during freezing; cover.
2. Strawberry Jam: Let stand at room temp. 24 hours until set.
3. Strawberry Jam: If not set after 24 hours, refrigerate for up to 3 weeks. Otherwise, store in freezer for up to 1 yr. Thaw in refrigerator.
4. Peach Jam: Same instructions as Strawberry Jam except fruit.
