ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 package (1 oz) dry ranch salad dressing mix"
      - "1 cup Miracle Whip"
      - "1 cup sour cream"
      - "1 1/2 tsp. dried dill weed"
      - "1/2 cup radishes, coarsely chopped"
      - "2 Tbsp. onion, finely chopped"
      - "1 medium carrot, grated"
---
1. In 1 quart bowl, combine salad dressing mix, Miracle Whip, sour cream, and dill weed; mix well.
2. Coarsely chop radishes and finely chop onion using food chopper.
3. Grate carrot using grater.
4. Stir radishes, onion, and carrot into mixture.
5. Chill and serve.
