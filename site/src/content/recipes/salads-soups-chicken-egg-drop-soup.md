ingredients:
  - items:
      - "6 cups rich chicken broth"
      - "2 Tbsp. cornstarch"
      - "2 Tbsp. cold water"
      - "1 Tbsp. soy sauce"
      - "1/2 tsp. sugar"
      - "2 eggs slightly beaten"
      - "2 green onions chopped (including tops)"
      - "salt and pepper"
---
1. In a large pan, heat chicken broth to boiling.
2. Blend cornstarch with water to make a smooth paste.
3. Stir in soy sauce and sugar.
4. Slowly stir into broth; bring to a boil and simmer until clear (stirring constantly).
5. Remove from heat.
6. Gradually add eggs, stirring until eggs separate into shreds.
7. Add green onions and season to taste.
8. Serve immediately.
