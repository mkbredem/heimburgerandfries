1. Place 6 eggs in a medium sauce pan and cover with water.
2. Bring to a boil and continue boiling for 15 minutes.
3. Immediately cover eggs with cold water and remove shells.
4. Next cut eggs into halves and place yolks into a bowl.
5. Then mash yolks, add enough pickle juice to make yolks moist and smooth.
6. Add about 1 Tbsp. sugar, a dash of salt and pepper and Miracle Whip (enough to make egg mixture creamy).
7. Stuff eggs.
