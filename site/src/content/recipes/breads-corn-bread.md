      - "2 cups flour"

      - "5 tsp. baking powder"
      - "1 tsp. salt"
      - "1 cup yellow cornmeal"
      - "2 eggs"
      - "2 cups milk"
---
1. Melt 1 stick of butter in a 9"x12" pan in the oven. Let cool in pan.
2. Sift together 2 cups flour, 1 cup sugar, 5 tsp. baking powder, and 1 tsp. salt. Add 1 cup yellow cornmeal.
3. Beat 2 eggs and stir in 2 cups milk. Add to flour.
4. Lastly, pour in melted butter from pan. Mix well.
5. Pour entire mixture back into 9"x12" pan and bake for 25 minutes at 400°F.
