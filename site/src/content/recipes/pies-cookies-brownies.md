      - "3/4 cup flour"
---
1. In an 8"x8" pan, place 1 stick oleo and 2 squares of semi-sweet chocolate and melt in the oven.
2. Let cool, then add 2 beaten eggs, 1 cup sugar, and 3/4 cup flour.
3. Stir mixture with a fork and bake for 30 min at 350°F.
