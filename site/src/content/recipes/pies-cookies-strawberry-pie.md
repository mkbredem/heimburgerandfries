ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 baked pie shell"
      - "1 1/2 cups crushed berries"
      - "1 cup sugar"
      - "3 Tbsp. cornstarch"
      - "1/2 cup water"
      - "Few drops lemon juice"
      - "1 1/2 cups whole fresh berries"
      - "Cool Whip"
---
1. Cook crushed berries, sugar, cornstarch, water, and lemon juice until transparent.
2. Cool and pour over 1 1/2 cups whole fresh berries in cooked pie shell.
3. Serve with Cool Whip.
