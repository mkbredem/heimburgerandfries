ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 pound ground sirloin"
      - "2 pound hot pork sausage"
      - "2 pounds Velveeta Cheese"
      - "1 bag shredded Mozzarella cheese"
      - "2 loaves party rye bread"
---
1. Brown meat and drain.
2. Cut Velveeta into chunks and melt, stirring often.
3. Mix melted cheese with meat mixture, 1 tsp. oregano, and mozzarella.
4. Spread on party rye.
5. Bake at 400°F for about 10-15 minutes.

**Note:** May place in freezer container with waxed paper between layers. Store and use as needed.
