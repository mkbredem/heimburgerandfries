ingredients:
  - items:
      - "2 cups sugar"
      - "2/3 cup condensed milk"
      - "1 cup peanut butter"
      - "1 7 oz. jar Marshmallow Creme"
---
1. Boil sugar and condensed milk until softball stage.
2. Remove from heat and add 1 cup peanut butter, and 1 7 oz. jar Marshmallow Creme.
3. Place in greased 9" x 11" pan.
4. Cool, cut into small squares.
5. Store in refrigerator until you serve it.
