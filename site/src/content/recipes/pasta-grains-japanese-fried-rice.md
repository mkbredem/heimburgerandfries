ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 cup sticky rice"
      - "1 tsp. chicken base"
      - "dash of Rosemary"
      - "1 cup frozen peas"
      - "1/3 cup finely chopped carrots"
      - "1/3 cup frozen corn"
      - "1 1/2 Tbsp. butter"
      - "2 Tbsp. soy sauce"
      - "salt & pepper"
---
1. Cook the rice according to directions, and add vegetables.
2. Melt butter in a large frying pan over medium/high heat, add rice mixture, soy sauce, and salt & pepper.
3. Cook 6-8 minutes stirring frequently.
