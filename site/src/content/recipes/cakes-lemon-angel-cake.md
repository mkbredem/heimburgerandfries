ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 pkg. angel food cake mix"
      - "2 lemons"
      - "2 cups powdered sugar"
      - "2 cups (16 oz) Cool Whip"
      - "10 oz raspberries"
---
1. Prepare angel food cake according to directions, and pour batter into 9"x13" pan, spreading evenly over bottom.
2. Bake on center rack for about 35-40 min. or until crust is golden brown and cracked.
3. Turn cake upside down and cool on cooling rack.
4. When cool, poke holes in cake about 1 1/2" deep and 1/2" apart with fork.
5. Zest 1 Tbsp. lemon zest, and squeeze lemons to yield 1/4 cup lemon juice.
6. Whisk together lemon juice & zest, and powdered sugar.
7. Pour glaze over cake and let stand 30 min.
8. To serve, garnish each slice with Cool Whip and raspberries.
