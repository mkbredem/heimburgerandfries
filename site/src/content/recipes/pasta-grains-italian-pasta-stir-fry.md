ingredients:
  - items:
      - "8 oz linguine - uncooked"
      - "2 medium tomatoes, seeded & chopped"
      - "1 medium onion, chopped"
      - "1 medium zucchini, sliced"
      - "1/4 cup fresh parsley, snipped"
      - "1 Tbsp. olive oil"
      - "2 garlic cloves, pressed"
      - "1/8 tsp. salt and pepper"
      - "1/4 cup fresh Parmesan, grated"
---
1. Cook linguine according to package directions, drain and keep warm.
2. Slice zucchini, and snip parsley.
3. Heat oil in stir-fry skillet until hot, and press garlic into skillet; stir-fry about 15 seconds.
4. Add zucchini and onion and stir-fry 2-3 minutes until vegetables are crisp-tender.
5. Add tomatoes, parsley, and seasonings; heat for 1-2 minutes stirring gently.
6. Remove from heat and stir in warm linguine.
7. Top with grated Parmesan cheese.
