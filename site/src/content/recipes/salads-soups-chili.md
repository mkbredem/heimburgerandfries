  - items:
      - "6 pounds ground sirloin"
      - "4 large sweet onions - chopped"
      - "3 - 24 oz. cans tomatoes - cut-up"
      - "5 cans tomato soup"
      - "6 cans chili beans"
      - "4 large cans tomato juice"
      - "salt & pepper"
      - "3 Tbsp. chili powder"
      - "2 cups water"
---
1. Brown together meat & onions.
2. Add 3 Tbsp. chili powder, & salt & pepper.
3. Drain mixture, add remaining ingredients and simmer for at least 4 hours.
