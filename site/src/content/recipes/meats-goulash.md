ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 pound ground sirloin"
      - "1 small onion - chopped finely"
      - "1 - 14 1/2 oz can whole tomatoes - chopped"
      - "1 - 10 3/4 oz can tomato sauce"
      - "1 1/4 cup elbow macaroni"
      - "water"
      - "salt & pepper to taste"
---
1. Brown sirloin with onion and drain off excess fat.
2. Add tomatoes and tomato sauce.
3. Add about 2 cups of water, salt and pepper, and simmer about 30 minutes.
4. Next, add 1 1/4 cup cooked macaroni, and cook another 30 minutes.
5. You may need to add more water and salt and pepper.
6. Top with Parmesan Cheese and serve.
