      - "1/2 cup chopped pecans"
  - group: "Cream Cheese Layer"
    items:
      - "4 oz. Cool Whip"
      - "8 oz. cream cheese"
      - "1 cup sifted powdered sugar"
  - group: "Pudding Layer"
    items:
      - "2 boxes banana or chocolate instant pudding"
      - "2 1/2 cups milk"
---
1. Crust: Pat in a 9"x12" pan, and bake for 20 min. at 350°F.
2. Cream Cheese Layer: Cream and spread over cooled crust.
3. Pudding Layer: Mix until thickened and spread over cream cheese mixture.
4. Top with Cool Whip, and top with crushed nuts.
