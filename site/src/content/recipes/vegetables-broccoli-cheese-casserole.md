      - "1 large can Cr. of Mushroom Soup"
      - "crushed Ritz Crackers"
---
1. Steam broccoli and cauliflower about 20-25 min.
2. Heat Cheese Whiz in microwave 2-3 min and mix together with mushroom soup.
3. Place cooked broccoli and cauliflower in greased [?]" x 13" Pyrex casserole.
4. Pour cheese mixture over it.
5. Top with thin layer of crushed Ritz Crackers.
6. Bake for about 1 hour (or until bubbly) at 350°F.
