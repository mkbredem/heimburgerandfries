1. Place drained potatoes in a large bowl.
2. Before adding squash, wait until it's cool enough to handle; then scrape squash meat from skin into bowl, using a large spoon. Discard the thyme.
3. Add the other half of garlic to bowl & pour in buttermilk.
4. Purée w/ a potato masher until desired consistency is reached. Add more buttermilk if necessary.
5. Season to taste w/ salt & black pepper.
6. Refrigerate in a covered container until ready to use.
7. To serve, reheat purée in microwave for 2-3 min on high heat. (Add more buttermilk if desired.)
8. Stir in chives just before serving.
