ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 cup margarine"
      - "2 cups packed light brown sugar"
      - "1/2 cup light corn syrup"
      - "1 tsp. salt"
      - "1/2 tsp. baking soda"
      - "1 tsp. vanilla"
      - "6 qt. (24 cups) popped corn"
---
1. Melt margarine in a large saucepan.
2. Stir in brown sugar, corn syrup, and salt, and bring to a boil, stirring constantly.
3. Then boil without stirring for 5 minutes.
4. Remove from heat and stir in soda and vanilla.
5. Pour syrup over popcorn and stir until popcorn is thoroughly coated.
6. Bake at 250°F for 1 hour (covered), stirring every 15 minutes.
7. Spread on waxed paper and allow to cool.
