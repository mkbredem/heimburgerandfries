ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 package frozen baby peas (cook as directed on package)"
      - "7 Tbsp. flour"
      - "2 cups milk"
      - "margarine (about 2 Tbsp.)"
      - "salt and pepper"
---
1. Cook peas as directed on package.
2. Place flour into saucepan and gradually add milk. Stir constantly until thickened.
3. Add peas to thickened mixture, add margarine (about 2 Tbsp.), and salt and pepper to taste.
