ingredients:
  - items:
      - "6-7 medium peppers (top cut-off, seeds removed)"
      - "1/2 cup dry white rice"
      - "1 pound ground sirloin"
      - "1 sweet onion - minced"
      - "2 eggs"
      - "12 oz can tomato paste"
      - "6 oz can tomato sauce"
      - "salt & pepper"
      - "water - add enough so that the mixture is the right consistency"
---
1. Before stuffing peppers, soak them in salt water for 1-2 hours.
2. Stuff peppers with meat mixture, cover with sauce, and bake for 1 1/2 hrs. at 350°F.
