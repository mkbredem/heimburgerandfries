      - "1 Round Steak (cut into bite-size pieces)"
      - "1 large onion - sliced"
      - "1 can bean sprouts"
      - "1 large can mushrooms"
      - "Bead molasses (Brown Gravy Sauce) - add until color is dark as desired"
---
1. Place meat in dutch oven and sear over low heat.
2. Add onion and celery and cook until meat draws its own juices.
3. Add bean sprouts and mushrooms.
4. Cover mixture with water, add salt, pepper, and bead molasses.
5. Cook slowly, adding water as necessary for about 5 hours.
6. Thicken with cornstarch before serving.
7. Serve over steamed white rice.
