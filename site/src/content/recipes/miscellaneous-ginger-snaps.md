ingredients:
  - items:
      - "2 cups sifted flour"
      - "2 tsp. baking soda"
      - "1 tsp. ginger"
      - "1 tsp. cloves"
      - "1 tsp. cinnamon"
      - "3/4 cup Crisco"
      - "1 cup sugar"
      - "4 Tbsp. molasses"
      - "1 egg"
---
1. Sift together all dry ingredients and set aside.
2. Cream shortening and add sugar, egg, and molasses. Beat well.
3. Add dry ingredients and beat mixture until smooth (mixture will be stiff).
4. Form mixture into 1" balls, roll in sugar, and bake on greased cookie sheets at 350°F for 10-12 minutes.
