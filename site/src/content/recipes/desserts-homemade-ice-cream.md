ingredients:
  - items:
      - "5 whole eggs"
      - "2 1/2 cups sugar"
      - "1/2 gallon whole milk"
      - "1 quart whipping cream"
      - "vanilla"
      - "2-3 bags crushed ice"
      - "ice cream salt"
      - "ice cream freezer - 6 qt. capacity"
---
1. Thoroughly mix eggs and sugar - about 10 minutes.
2. Add whipping cream and mix another 5-10 minutes.
3. Place mixture in ice cream freezer, and add milk and vanilla (it takes a lot of vanilla).
4. Finish ice cream according to freezer directions.
