ingredients:
  - items:
      - "3 eggs - well beaten"
      - "2 cups brown sugar (1 package)"
      - "3 cups buttermilk"
      - "4 1/2 cups flour"
      - "1 1/2 tsp. soda"
      - "1 1/2 tsp. baking powder"
      - "3/4 tsp salt"
      - "1 cup walnuts (black)"
---
1. Beat eggs and sugar together.
2. Add milk, then remaining ingredients.
3. Bake in greased/floured loaf pans at 325°F for 45-50 minutes.
