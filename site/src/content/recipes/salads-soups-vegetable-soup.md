ingredients:
  - items:
      - "5 lbs. London Broil or Chuck Roast"
      - "1 large sweet onion, sliced"
      - "1/2 pot (large stock pot) water"
  - group: "Add"
    items:
      - "2 large cans tomato juice"
      - "2 cans cut green beans - drained"
      - "2 cans cut waxed beans - drained"
      - "1 package frozen corn (may use fresh)"
      - "1 package frozen lima beans (optional)"
      - "1 head of cabbage - shredded"
      - "4 large potatoes - diced"
      - "2 cups diced carrots"
---
1. Cook about 2 hours or until meat is tender.
2. Remove cooked meat and cut into bite-sized pieces.
3. Add tomato juice, green beans, waxed beans, corn, lima beans, cabbage, potatoes, and carrots.
4. Simmer soup at least 4 hours.
