ingredients:
  - items:
      - "12 cups water - 20 beef bouillon cubes"
      - "10 medium onions - sliced"
      - "1 stick margarine"
      - "1-2 Tbsp. flour"
      - "1 loaf of French bread (narrow width)"
      - "6 oz. Swiss cheese"
      - "1 oz. Provolone cheese"
      - "6 oz. Mozzarella cheese"
      - "3 oz. Muenster cheese"
      - "3 oz. Camembert cheese"
      - "3 oz. Brick cheese"
      - "1/2 cup sherry"
      - "butter"
      - "Parmesan cheese"
---
1. Saute onions in margarine until clear.
2. Add flour, then beef bouillon.
3. Simmer about 1 hour. Then add sherry.
4. Grate all cheeses and mix together.
5. Slice bread, butter on both sides, sprinkle with Parmesan cheese, and broil until brown.
6. Divide onion mixture into 8 individual baking dishes.
7. Place toast on top and add cheeses.
8. Place baking dishes on cookie sheet and bake for 1 hour at 350°F.
