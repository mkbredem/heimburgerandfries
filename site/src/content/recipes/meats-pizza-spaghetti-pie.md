ingredients:
  - group: "Meat Layer"
    items:
      - "1 lb ground sirloin"
      - "1/2 cup bread crumbs"
      - "1/2 cup chopped onion"
      - "1 tsp. salt"
      - "dash of pepper"
      - "5.3 oz evaporated milk"
  - group: "Spaghetti Layer"
    items:
      - "4 oz cooked spaghetti"
      - "1 egg beaten"
      - "1/4 cup grated parmesan"
      - "2 T butter"
  - group: "Topping"
    items:
      - "8 oz spaghetti sauce"
      - "1 cup shredded mozzarella"
      - "parmesan"
---
1. Meat Layer: Mix ground sirloin, bread crumbs, onion, salt, pepper, and evaporated milk. Bake at 350°F for 35-40 minutes.
2. Spaghetti Layer: Mix cooked spaghetti, beaten egg, grated parmesan, and butter, and put on meat layer.
3. Topping: Add 8 oz spaghetti sauce to spaghetti layer, 1 cup shredded mozzarella & sprinkle [?] parmesan.
4. Bake for 10 minutes.
5. Let stand for 5 minutes before cutting.
