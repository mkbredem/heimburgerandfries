1. Chicken: In deep frying pan fill up oil 1/3 way up pan. Make sure oil is real hot.
2. Chicken: Dip chicken in flour, salt & pepper mixture real well.
3. Chicken: If oil is really hot, cook 6 min. on each side.
4. Gravy: Pour oil (use ladle or measuring cup) in bowl; leave enough oil to cover bottom of pan.
5. Gravy: Put salt & pepper in pan, put enough flour to soak grease up.
6. Gravy: Then add a little milk (keep stirring lumps out).
7. Gravy: Add milk to consistency.
