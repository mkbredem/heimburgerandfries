ingredients:
  - items:
      - "2 lbs butternut squash (one medium), cut in half lengthwise, seeds removed"
      - "1 teaspoon olive oil"
      - "Coarse-ground salt, to taste"
      - "Cracked black pepper, to taste"
      - "4-6 cloves garlic, peeled & cut in half lengthwise"
      - "2 sprigs thyme"
      - "2 pounds Yukon gold or russet potatoes, peeled & cut into 1 1/2 to 2-inch chunks"
      - "1 - 1 1/2 cups nonfat buttermilk"
      - "2 tablespoons chives, minced"
---
1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
2. Brush squash's cut surfaces w/ olive oil. Season w/ salt & black pepper.
3. Place half the garlic & 1 sprig of thyme on a cookie sheet & cover w/ a squash half, cut side down. Do the same with the other squash half.
4. Bake until completely soft & tender, about 25-35 min.
5. Meanwhile, in a large stockpot, place potatoes & cover w/ cold salted water.
6. Bring water to a boil; then reduce to a simmer & cook until a fork goes through potatoes easily, approximately 25-30 min.
7. Place drained potatoes in a large bowl.
8. Before adding squash, wait until it's cool enough to handle; then scrape squash meat from skin into bowl, using a large spoon. Discard the thyme.
9. Add the other half of garlic to bowl & pour in buttermilk.
10. Purée w/ a potato masher until desired consistency is reached. Add more buttermilk if necessary.
11. Season to taste w/ salt & black pepper.
12. Refrigerate in a covered container until ready to use.
13. To serve, reheat purée in microwave for 2-3 min on high heat. (Add more buttermilk if desired.)
14. Stir in chives just before serving.
