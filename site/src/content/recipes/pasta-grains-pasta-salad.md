ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 pound cooked spaghetti"
      - "3/4 jar Salad Supreme"
      - "1 bottle Italian Dressing"
      - "1 red onion (diced)"
      - "1 cucumber (diced)"
      - "1 red pepper, chopped finely"
      - "1 green pepper, chopped finely"
      - "1 yellow pepper, chopped finely"
      - "2 tomatoes - chopped"
---
1. Add Salad Supreme to cooked spaghetti, and mix.
2. Then add Italian Dressing and mix.
3. Next, add chopped vegetables and mix.
4. Cover and refrigerate.
