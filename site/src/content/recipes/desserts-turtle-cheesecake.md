ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 - Graham Cracker Crust"
      - "2 - 8 oz. packages Philadelphia Cream Cheese"
      - "2 eggs"
      - "1/2 cup sugar"
      - "2 Tbsp. milk"
      - "caramel topping"
      - "1/2 cup chopped pecans"
      - "1/2 tsp. vanilla"
      - "20 caramels"
---
1. Cream cheese, sugar, and vanilla with electric mixer until well blended.
2. Add eggs and mix until well blended; set aside.
3. Melt caramels with milk on low heat, stirring frequently until smooth.
4. Stir in pecans.
5. Pour caramel and pecan mixture into graham cracker crust.
6. Top with cheese batter.
7. Bake at 350°F for about 40 minutes or until knife comes out of center clean.
8. Chill at least 3 hours.
