ingredients:
  - items:
      - "2 cups brown sugar (1 box)"
      - "2 cups white sugar"
      - "2 cups vegetable oil"
      - "4 eggs"
      - "2 tsp. vanilla"
  - group: "Add"
    items:
      - "4 cups flour"
      - "2 cups oats"
      - "4 cups cornflakes"
      - "2 tsp. soda"
      - "1 tsp. salt"
      - "1 - 12 oz. pkg. chocolate chips"
---
1. Mix brown sugar, white sugar, vegetable oil, eggs, and vanilla thoroughly with beater.
2. Add flour, oats, cornflakes, soda, salt, and chocolate chips.
3. Drop by teaspoonfuls onto parchment covered baking sheets; bake at 350°F for 10-14 minutes.
4. Cool on baking rack.
