ingredients:
  - group: "1st Part"
    items:
      - "4 tbsp flour"
      - "1 cup milk"
  - group: "2nd Part"
    items:
      - "1/2 cup butter"
      - "1/2 cup white shortening"
      - "1 cup powdered sugar"
      - "1 tsp vanilla"
---
1. 1st Part: Place flour in saucepan & gradually add milk.
2. 1st Part: Stir till smooth; use egg beater if necessary.
3. 1st Part: Cook until thick, stirring constantly.
4. 1st Part: Cook, set & cool (skim the top when ready to use). Cool before adding to last mixture.
5. 2nd Part: Cream butter & shortening for 4 min.
6. 2nd Part: Add powdered sugar; cream for 4 min.
7. 2nd Part: Add flour mixture & cream for 4 more min. Mix - beat the life out of it.
8. 2nd Part: Add vanilla, mix & spread.
