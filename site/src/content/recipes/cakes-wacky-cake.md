1. Cake: Sift flour, sugar, cocoa, salt and soda together in a baking pan.
2. Cake: Make 3 holes & add vanilla, vinegar and cooking oil.
3. Cake: Cover with 1 cup cold water & mix thoroughly with a fork.
4. Cake: Bake @ 350°F for [?] min.
5. Icing: Melt butter & chocolate together; add to powdered sugar.
6. Icing: Add milk & vanilla and mix.
