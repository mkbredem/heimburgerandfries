ingredients:
  - items:
      - "3 pound piece of London Broil"
      - "1 medium onion - sliced"
      - "1 1/2 heads cabbage - cut into wedges"
      - "6-7 potatoes cut into halves"
---
1. Place meat and onion into a large pot, and add about 1/2 pot of water.
2. Cook over medium heat about 1 1/2 hours to 2 hours, or until tender.
3. When meat is tender, add cabbage wedges and potatoes.
4. Continue cooking another 1 1/2 hours over medium heat.
