---
1. Place sirloin tip roast in large roaster pan (uncovered) in 400°F oven for 1 hour.
2. Turn roast, apply salt, pepper, & garlic powder, cover, reduce heat to 325°F and cook for several hours. [Reserve meat drippings.]
3. After roast cools, trim fat off it and slice.
4. To meat drippings, add 2 packages of McCormick's Au Jus (prepared according to package directions).
5. Heat thoroughly and add sliced meat.
6. To serve, place meat on Chicago Rolls, and place juice in small individual bowls.
