ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1/4 cup canola oil"
      - "1 medium onion thinly sliced"
      - "1/2 medium green pepper (or red) chopped"
      - "1 cup regular rice uncooked"
      - "2 - 8 oz. cans Tomato Sauce"
      - "1 3/4 cups hot water"
      - "1 tsp. salt, dash pepper"
      - "1/2 pound ground sirloin"
---
1. Heat oil in a skillet, and add onion, peppers, beef, and rice.
2. Stir over medium high heat until lightly browned.
3. Add tomato sauce and remaining ingredients. Mix well and quickly bring to a boil.
4. Cover and simmer for 25 minutes.

**Note:** May add 1/2 cup shredded Parmesan cheese before serving.
