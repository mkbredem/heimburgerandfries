1. Cake: Follow directions on cake box & add lemon pudding; mix and follow baking directions on box.
2. Cake: While hot, poke holes in cake & pour topping over cake.
3. Glaze: Mix and pour over warm lemon cake. (Use a fork & stick holes in lemon cake.)
