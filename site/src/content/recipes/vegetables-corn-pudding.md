ingredients:
  - items:
      - "2 eggs, slightly beaten"
      - "1 can cream style corn"
      - "1 can whole kernel corn - drained"
      - "1/2 cup milk"
      - "3 Tbsp. sugar"
      - "1 small box corn bread mix"
      - "1 stick of butter"
---
1. Mix all ingredients together.
2. Grease pan (use Pam) and pour mixture into a pan.
3. Melt 1 stick of butter and pour over the top.
4. Bake in 8" x 10" pan for 1 hour at 325°F. Bake less if top gets too brown.
