ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1/2 cup butter - 1 stick"
      - "1 box powdered sugar"
      - "2 cups crunchy peanut butter"
      - "3 cups Rice Krispies"
      - "6 oz. chocolate chips"
      - "1/2 bar of paraffin"
---
1. Mix above ingredients (with mixer), and shape into small balls.
2. In a double-boiler place 6 oz. of chocolate chips and 1/2 bar of paraffin - melt.
3. Dip peanut butter balls into chocolate mixture (using a fork) and place on wax paper to cool.
4. Store in container in the refrigerator.
