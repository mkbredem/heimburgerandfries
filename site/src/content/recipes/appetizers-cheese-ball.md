ingredients:
  - items:
      - "2 - 8 oz. packages cream cheese"
      - "4 green onions - chopped finely"
      - "1 1/2 Tbsp. Accent"
      - "1 1/2 Tbsp. Worcestershire"
      - "1 package dried beef - cut up (Buddig)"
---
1. Mix all ingredients except 1/2 cup chopped dried beef.
2. Shape mixture into a ball and sprinkle remaining dried beef on the top.
3. Chill and serve with crackers.
