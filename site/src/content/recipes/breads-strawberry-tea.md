ingredients:
  - items:
      - "3 cups flour"
      - "1 tsp. baking soda"
      - "1 tsp. salt"
      - "1 1/4 cup chopped pecans"
      - "2 pts. fresh strawberries - mashed"
      - "1 1/4 cup melted butter"
      - "4 eggs"
      - "2 cups sugar"
---
1. Beat eggs well, add sugar & mix.
2. Add butter & mashed strawberries.
3. Add mixture to sifted dry ingredients; mix.
4. Pour into 2 greased loaf pans and bake about 1 hour at 350°F.
5. Cool in pan for about 10 minutes.
