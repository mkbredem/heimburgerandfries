ingredients:
  - items:
      - "2 packages strawberry Jello"
      - "1 cup boiling water"
      - "2 - 10 oz. frozen or fresh sliced strawberries"
      - "1 - large can crushed pineapple"
      - "3 medium bananas - mashed"
      - "1 cup chopped walnuts"
      - "1 pint sour cream"
---
1. Place jello in large bowl and add boiling water, stirring until Jello is dissolved.
2. Next, fold in strawberries, pineapple, bananas, and nuts.
3. Turn 1/2 of strawberry mixture into a 13" x 9" baking dish. Refrigerate until firm and spread with sour cream.
4. Gently spoon on remaining strawberry mixture.
5. Refrigerate.
