1. Boil potatoes until soft.
2. Mash potatoes with milk.
3. Add approx. 16 oz. [sour cream]. Add salt, pepper & chives to taste.
4. Stir potatoes, sour cream & chives until mixed well.
5. Pour into 1 1/2 quart casserole dish (greased), & place 4 slices of butter on top.
6. Bake @ 350°F for 1 hour.
