      - "1/2 cup water"
      - "1/2 cup melted butter or margarine"
      - "1 cup sugar"
      - "2 tsp. cinnamon"
---
1. Dissolve yeast with 1/4 cup warm water; let stand for 10 minutes.
2. Mix Bisquick with 1/2 cup water; add to yeast. Dough should be soft, but not gooey.
3. Divide dough into fourths and roll each into a rectangular shape about 1/4" thick.
4. Brush with melted butter.
5. Mix together sugar and cinnamon and sprinkle over butter.
6. Roll in jellyroll fashion. Cut into 1" slices and place into greased pans (either muffin pans or 8" cake pans).
7. Brush top with remaining butter and sprinkle with sugar and cinnamon.
8. Bake at 350°F for 20 minutes.
