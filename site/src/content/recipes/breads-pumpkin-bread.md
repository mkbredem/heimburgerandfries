ingredients:
  - items:
      - "3 cups sugar"
      - "1 cup shortening"
      - "4 eggs"
      - "1/2 tsp salt"
      - "1 tsp. cinnamon"
      - "1 tsp. nutmeg"
      - "3 1/2 cups flour"
      - "2/3 cups water"
      - "2 tsp. soda"
      - "2 cups pumpkin"
      - "1 cup raisins"
---
1. Mix sugar & shortening.
2. Beat in eggs.
3. Add remaining ingredients and mix well.
4. Place mixture into 3 greased & floured loaf pans.
5. Bake for 45-50 minutes at 350°F.
