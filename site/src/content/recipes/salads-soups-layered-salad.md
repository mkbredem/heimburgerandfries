ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 medium head of lettuce"
      - "1/2 cup chopped celery"
      - "1/2 cup green peppers"
      - "1 medium Spanish onion"
      - "2 cups Miracle Whip"
      - "1 pkg. uncooked frozen peas"
      - "2 Tbsp. sugar"
      - "4 oz. package shredded Colby cheese"
      - "8 slices bacon, cooked crisply & crumbled"
---
1. Layer as listed above (except mix Miracle Whip and sugar together before covering): lettuce, celery, green peppers, onions, and peas.
2. Cover with Miracle Whip mixture, then cheese, and finally cooked bacon.
3. Cover with foil and refrigerate for 24 hours.
