ingredients:
  - group: "Cake"
    items:
      - "2 cups sugar"
      - "4 eggs"
      - "2 tsp. salt"
      - "2 tsp. cinnamon"
      - "3 cups coarsely grated carrots"
      - "1 cup black walnuts - finely chopped"
      - "1 1/2 cups oil"
      - "2 1/4 cups flour"
      - "2 tsp. soda"
  - group: "Icing"
    items:
      - "1/2 box powdered sugar"
      - "8 oz cream cheese"
---
1. Cake: Combine sugar, oil, & eggs; mix.
2. Cake: Add remaining dry ingredients and mix.
3. Cake: Finally, add carrots and nuts and mix.
4. Cake: Pour into greased jelly roll pan (9x12) and bake at 300°F for 1 1/2 hours.
5. Icing: Mix powdered sugar and cream cheese and spread.
