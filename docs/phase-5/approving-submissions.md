# Approving family submissions

When someone uses "Share your version," "Share your dish" or "Submit a new recipe" on heimburgerandfries.com, their answers arrive as a new row in the Google Sheet **Share a dish — responses** (in Michael's Google Drive folder **Heimburger & Fries submissions**). Nothing appears on the website until a row is approved and imported.

## To review a submission

1. Open the **Share a dish — responses** sheet. Michael shares it with each approver.
2. Read the new row. Photos appear as links in the photo columns at the far right; click a link to see the photo.
3. In the **Status** column, choose:
   - **Approved** — it belongs on the site.
   - **Rejected** — it doesn't (a duplicate, a test, or something that isn't a recipe).
4. That's all. Leave the **Imported** column alone. If it later shows a message starting with "Error," tell Michael.

## What happens next

Within about 15 minutes, a script adds every approved row to the website and writes the date in the **Imported** column. The website updates a few minutes after that. Approved changes and photos appear on the recipe's page under "Family versions & photos," and approved new recipes appear in **Family Additions**. Only the submitter's first name is shown.

## Good to know

- To fix a typo in a submission before it's imported, edit the cell directly in the sheet.
- To take something down after it's on the site, tell Michael which recipe and which entry.
